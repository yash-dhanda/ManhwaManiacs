### C. Dock, search orb and bottom accessory (`shell/Dock.tsx`, `dock-math.ts`, `SearchOrb.tsx`, `Accessory.tsx`, `dock-menus.ts`, `accessory-store.ts`, §7.15)

1. **Dock:** a `glassRegular` (T3) capsule 64 tall, 21 px from the left and bottom (+ safe-bottom) edges, its right edge leaving room for the 50 px orb and an 8 px gap before the 21 px inset. 2. **Droplet:** a `glassFilm` clear capsule 56 × 52 under the active tab, drawn as a clear-finish region of the dock's own map (never its own `backdrop-filter`). Tapping another tab moves it on `tab` (k 195.0, c 21.78) with the stretch `scaleX = 1 + min(|v| / 2000, 0.25)`, `scaleY = 1 / √scaleX`, and fires `nav.change`. Dragging across the dock (press on the droplet or anywhere on the dock and slide): the droplet lifts (fully clear, +6 %), follows the finger on `track`, fires `nav.scrub` over each tab, rubber-bands at most 18 px past the first and last tab, and on release projects (`project()`) to the nearest tab and settles on `tab`; a touch catches it in flight (`motion.catch`). While travelling or dragged, the group uses the base map and the droplet draws `glassFilm` clear's fill, rim and inner light only; on settle the cached map for the new tab returns. The droplet takes the field's rim tint at 12 % (Light follows the story).
3. **Metaball:** dragging the droplet past the last tab toward the orb bridges the two with a neck when the gap is under 12 px (the neck is added to the group's mask only, no displacement); releasing on the orb opens search.
4. **Minimise:** after 20 px of cumulative downward scroll (`threshold.dockHide`) the dock morphs on `minimize` (k 246.7, c 31.42) into a 50 px capsule showing only the active tab's icon; the orb stays; 12 px of upward scroll (`threshold.dockShow`), reaching the top of a list, a tap on the capsule, or focus on one of its tab nodes restores it. Reversing the scroll mid-morph reverses it with its current velocity. It never minimises while `html[data-sr="on"]`. Minimised, all four tab nodes stay in the tree, visually hidden with `clip-path` (never `display: none`).
5. **Badges:** Home an 8 px `iris400` dot when `useUnreadNotificationCount()` is above 0; Library a count badge of active downloads (queued + downloading + failed from the shared queue); You a `bloom` dot while `useLetters()` has a letter in state `new`.

A tap stretches it along `morph` into the `web/26` `SearchField variant="bottom"` (50 tall `glassRegular`, full width minus 21 px insets, riding the keyboard by the visual-viewport rule) while the dock sinks away on `minimize`, and pushes `/search` (the Search screen above the field is `web/38`; the field writes `?q=` with `router.replace`, debounced 300 ms, `Enter` at once).
