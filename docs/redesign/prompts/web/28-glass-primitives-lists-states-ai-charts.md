### B. Swipe row actions (`primitives/list/SwipeRow.tsx`, `swipe-math.ts`, §7.34, §4.6)

1. **Only on coarse pointers** (`matchMedia("(pointer: coarse)")`): `touch-action: pan-y` on the row, Motion `drag="x"` with `dragDirectionLock`, `dragElastic={0.1}`, `dragMomentum={false}`. A drag starts only after 10 px (`threshold.dragSlopTouch`) of horizontal movement and never when the pointer went down within 24 px of the left screen edge (the iOS Safari back swipe, §8.0.8). The row tracks the finger 1:1.
2. **Actions:** props `leading` and `trailing`, each `SwipeAction[]` = `{id, label, glyph, tone: "iris" | "success" | "warning" | "danger" | "neutral", destructive?, run(): void | Promise<void>, undo?(): Promise<void>}`; the first action of a side is its full-swipe action.
3. **Pills:** behind the row, separate pills 44 tall in 88 px slots, drawn as content twins; each inflates from scale 0.6 to 1.0 as it is revealed (`scale = 0.6 + 0.4 × clamp(reveal / 88, 0, 1)`), glyph 20 Regular and label `caption1` 600 in the action's colour (`iris400` `#A99BFF`, `success` `#3DDC84`, `warning` `#FFB547`, `danger` `#FF5C5C`, `label1` for neutral).
4. **Release:** the projected offset (`project()`, §4.4) past half the tray width (`n × 88 / 2`) opens the tray on `snappy`; otherwise it closes on `settle`. One open tray per list; tapping the row, scrolling or opening another tray closes it; tapping a pill runs it.
5. **Full swipe:** a projected offset past 60 % of the row width (`threshold.rowCommitFraction`) stretches the leading pill to fill the gap and turns it solid in its colour (black label); `threshold.cross` fires when the projection crosses the line and `threshold.back` when it crosses back (the pill re-shrinks). Commit: the row slides out on `dismiss` with the release velocity, the list closes the gap on `snappy`, and the action runs; a destructive action shows the 10 s Undo toast (`showToast({ undo })`, `web/27`) instead of a confirm; a non-destructive one (mark read) lets the row spring back on `settle` once it ran.
6. **Alternatives (never gesture-only, §11, §14.8):** every row has a ⋯ plain icon button (44, `aria-label="More actions for {row name}"`) opening a `web/27` `Menu` with every swipe action; on `(hover: hover)` devices the actions also appear as plain icon buttons at the trailing edge on row hover and `:focus-within`; with the row focused, keys run the actions whose ids are `markRead` (`m`), `download` (`d`), `remove` (`Delete`, and `Backspace` when focus is not in a field) and `u` undoes the last one while its toast shows.
7. **Reduced motion:** tracking stays 1:1; release cross-fades to the outcome over 150 ms; pills appear at scale 1.
8. `swipe-math.ts` (tested): pill scale by reveal, open or close by projection, the full-swipe line at 0.6 × width projected, the 24 px edge rule.

### C. Drag to reorder (`primitives/list/ReorderList.tsx`, `reorder-math.ts`, §7.35)

1. **Lift:** a 450 ms press on a row opens the `web/27` context preview (`useContextPreview(el, "row")`: the row at 1.02, `dimContext`, the menu, `longpress.open`); dragging the preview more than 10 px closes the menu, fades `dimContext` out over 180 ms and turns the preview into the reorder lift: scale 1.02 → 1.03 on `press`, the rim brightens to `rgba(255,255,255,0.30)`, shadow `0 12px 28px rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`, `reorder.lift`. A trailing handle (plain icon 44, `dots-six-vertical`, `aria-label="Reorder {name}"`, `data-cursor="grab"` and `grabbing` while dragged, the `web/26` §7.40 cursor contract) starts the lift at once, with no menu.
2. **Drag:** neighbours part around the gap on `snappy`; `reorder.pass` (a `select` tick) each time the item passes a slot; drop settles on `snappy` with `reorder.drop` (`soft(0.5)`) and calls `onReorder(from, to)` (screens write the order: source pins, collections, collection members, manual library order, the download queue, profiles). `layout="grid"` maps the pointer to a cell by the grid's measured column count.
3. **Edge auto-scroll:** inside the 64 px edge zone of the scroll container, `speed = 1200 px/s × depthIntoZone / 64`.
4. **Keyboard:** `alt+↑` / `alt+↓` move one slot; `alt+shift+←` / `alt+shift+→` move one slot horizontally in grids; `alt+shift+↑` / `alt+shift+↓` move to the top or bottom; bare `alt+←/→` is never bound. Each move is announced in an `aria-live="assertive"` region: "Solo Leveling moved to position 3 of 12". Every row's ⋯ menu carries "Move up", "Move down", "Move to top", "Move to bottom" (WCAG 2.5.7).
5. **Reduced motion:** tracking stays 1:1; neighbours jump; the drop cross-fades over 150 ms.
6. `reorder-math.ts` (tested): the target slot from the pointer in a list and in a grid, the auto-scroll speed at 0, 32 and 64 px, keyboard moves clamped at both ends.

### D. Bulk selection and the floating bar (`primitives/select/useSelectMode.ts`, `SelectableGroup.tsx`, `select-paint.ts`, `BulkToolbar.tsx`, `FloatingBar.tsx`, `bulk-copy.ts`, §7.35)
