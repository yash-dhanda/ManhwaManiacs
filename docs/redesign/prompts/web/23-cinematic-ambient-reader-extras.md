### A. Engine work (`frontend/src/features/reader/`, skin-neutral, no pixels)

Vitest beside every module (the `node` environment; only `src/**/*.test.ts` runs).

1. **`page-tint.ts`**: `hls(r, g, b)` and `pickPageSeed(rgba: Uint8ClampedArray): string | null` implementing §2.1.5 exactly (keep pixels with 0.10 < L < 0.90; the seed is the kept pixel with the highest S, ties to the first in row-major order; greyscale when no kept pixel has S ≥ 0.08 or none is kept; the result upper-case `#RRGGBB`); `TintTracker` (pure): feed it `seed | null` per sampled page and it returns the chrome's tint source: the new seed for a chromatic page, the previous one for a greyscale page, and `"cover"` after **6 consecutive greyscale pages** until a chromatic page arrives. The **parity test** lives in `frontend/src/skins/cinematic/tint.test.ts` (the file the contract names, beside web/04's contrast loops): it reads `../../../../design/tint-vectors.json` and asserts `pickPageSeed` returns every `expected`. `page-tint.test.ts` covers `TintTracker` (5 greyscale keeps the colour, the 6th switches to `"cover"`, a chromatic page switches back).
2. **`panels.ts`**: `luminanceOf(rgba)` (the Rec. 709 formula on 8-bit values), `detectPanels(lum: Uint8Array, width, height, direction): Rect[]` in px at the analysis width, implementing §9.4.3 steps 1–7 and the three A0 interpretations: a row (or, within a band, a column) is a gutter line when ≥ 98 % of its pixels have L > 0.92 or ≥ 98 % have L < 0.06; a gutter is a run of ≥ 12 gutter lines; content runs between gutters (shorter gutter runs stay inside content) become bands; each band is split once at its vertical gutters (skipped when height / width > 2, where a band is one full-width rect); rects under 48 px on either side are dropped; order top to bottom, then left to right, or right to left for `rtl`. `toPageFractions(rects, width, height)` for the manifest shape. **`panels.test.ts`** reads `design/panel-vectors.json` and each PNG (a 20-line test-only decoder: check the signature, read IHDR, `zlib.inflateSync` the concatenated IDAT data, strip the filter byte of each row after asserting it is 0) and asserts exact equality with `expected` for all 24 cases.
3. **`page-tint.worker.ts`** (§15.2: `new Worker(new URL("./page-tint.worker.ts", import.meta.url), { type: "module" })`): one message per analysis request, `{id, tint?: ImageBitmap /* 16 × 16 */, panels?: {bitmap: ImageBitmap /* 360 wide */, direction}}` → `{id, seed?: string | null, tintFailed?: true, panels?: Rect[], panelsFailed?: true}`. It draws each bitmap into an `OffscreenCanvas` (`imageSmoothingQuality = "medium"`; downsampling to 16 × 16 itself if the received bitmap is not 16 × 16), reads the pixels, runs `pickPageSeed` and `detectPanels`, and calls `bitmap.close()` on both. Tint and the next page's panels travel **in the same message**, so a 10 s scroll never posts more than 17 messages (web/24 counts them).
4. **`page-analysis.ts`** (main thread): creates the worker lazily on first use; `analysePage({tintImg?, panelImg?, direction})` makes the bitmaps from the decoded `<img>`s with `createImageBitmap(img, { resizeWidth: 16, resizeHeight: 16, resizeQuality: "medium" })` and `createImageBitmap(img, { resizeWidth: 360, resizeQuality: "medium" })` (height proportional) and transfers them; a `SecurityError` or any decode failure returns `tintFailed` / `panelsFailed` (the cover-colour fallback of §9.4.5). Proof and production run same-origin through the `/api` rewrite, so pages are never tainted.
5. **Engine duties** (in the engine hook, `useReaderEngine()`; the §15.4 list):
   - **Page tint**: `pageTint: {source: "page", seed} | {source: "cover"} | null` in state (null = page tint off or nothing sampled yet). The manifest's `pages[].tint` is used first (tinted from frame 0); otherwise the page under the reading line (`pageAtReadingLine()`: 38 % from the top in the strip; the current page in paged and guided layouts) is sampled when it changes, **at most one sample per 600 ms** (`dur.sample.tint`), the moment it decodes (the first read of a new chapter is tinted too), through `TintTracker`. Tint failures use the cover source.
   - **Panel detection**: `panels: Record<page, PanelRect[] | "finding" | "none">` in page fractions; the manifest's `pages[].panels` first (an empty list means "none"), then the local manifest copy of a saved chapter, then analysis **one page ahead** of the reading position, piggybacked on the tint messages; entering guided view on a page with no result analyses that page at once (`"finding"` meanwhile).

7. **Auto-scroll model** (on top of web/12's `autoScrollPxPerSecondX`), pure functions in `auto-scroll.ts` with tests:
   - Manga: `px/s = 60 × speed × viewportHeight / 1080` (a 844 px phone at 1.00× scrolls 46.9 px/s); speed 0.50–3.00 in 0.05 steps.
   - **Ramp**: every change of the effective speed (start, a speed step, a pace change) eases from the current rate to the new one over 400 ms (`dur.glide`) along `ease.settle` `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`; integration stays elapsed-time based (the existing `autoScrollFrameDistance` with the 100 ms frame clamp), so dropped frames never change the pace.
   - **Pace by dialogue**: `paceFactor(words) = clamp(1.2 − words / 60, 0.5, 1.0)` (the `scalar.pace.*` tokens: 12 words or fewer → 1.0, 42 words → 0.5, a wordless page → 1.0); recomputed every 250 ms and eased over 400 ms; applies only when the chapter has dialogue text.
   - **Interruption**: a touch pauses; release resumes after 800 ms when "Resume after I let go" is on; a wheel, key or drag scroll pauses and stays paused; the chrome stays hidden.
   - **Boundaries**: continuous mode rolls through seams and read-all dividers, pausing only at the `CH 143 IS ON ITS WAY` band (web/12) and resuming when its pages land; at the series' end it stops, fires `autoscroll.end` and shows the chrome.
   - **States**: `off | running | paused`; `p`, the folio-bar button and the chip toggle `running` ↔ `paused` (from `off` they start); leaving the reader, a paged layout, a navigated chapter change or the series' end set `off`. Reduced motion never auto-starts it (nothing in this step starts it by itself) and it stays available by hand.
   - K39 levels migrate through web/12's formula; keep that test green.
8. **Novel pace** (`frontend/src/features/novels/pace.ts` + test): the profile's pace is the median, over its last 10 completed novel chapters, of `word_count / (time_spent_seconds / 60)`, counting only chapters with 60 ≤ `time_spent_seconds` ≤ 7200 and a pace of 80–900 wpm, and 250 wpm until 3 samples exist; stored in scoped `localStorage` `mm.novel-pace` as `{samples: [{chapter_key, wpm}]}` (at most 10), recomputed on each chapter completion from the seconds the reading clock pushed for that chapter; `novelPxPerSecond(paceWpm, speed, lineHeightPx, avgWordsPerLine) = (paceWpm × speed / 60) × lineHeightPx / avgWordsPerLine`, where `avgWordsPerLine` = the laid-out chapter's word count ÷ its rendered line count, recomputed after any Type sheet change.

### C. House sound (`frontend/src/skins/cinematic/soundscape.ts`, §9.4.2)

1. **Mixer** (one per page): an `AudioContext` created on the first user gesture in a reader; per loop an `AudioBufferSourceNode` with `loop = true` → a `GainNode` (volume × duck) → destination. `setLoop(id | null)` cross-fades (old out over 600 ms, new in over 2000 ms); `fadeIn()` 2000 ms and `fadeOut()` 600 ms with `linearRampToValueAtTime`; `setVolume(v)`; `duck(on)` ramps to 30 % of the volume (or back) over 300 ms; `pause()` / `resume()` with those fades. Decoded buffers are kept in memory by id. While a loop plays, set the flag `skins/cinematic/sounds.ts` reads to suppress UI cues (add `setSoundscapeActive(bool)` there if it is missing).
2. **Files and cache**: the URL is `{API base}/app/soundscapes/{id}.{ext}` (the API base from the same resolver `services/http.ts` uses; `/api/app/soundscapes/…` in production) with ids `projector-room, rain-on-glass, night-city, cafe, night-wind, low-drone, afternoon-park, temple-bells`; `ext` is `ogg` when `new Audio().canPlayType('audio/ogg; codecs="vorbis"')` is non-empty, else `m4a` (narration keeps web/15's Opus probe). `public/sw.js` gains a cache-first rule for GET requests whose path contains `/app/soundscapes/`, stored in the Cache Storage cache `mm-soundscapes-v1` (the name backend/07 documents; shared with Glass; not per profile, because the files are not user data), so a loop heard once plays offline.
3. **Choice**: `OFF`, `MATCH THE MOOD` or a loop id, per profile (the field web/13 or web/18 created in `mm.reader-settings` and `mm.novel-settings`; add `soundscape: "off" | "match" | <id>` where missing, default `"off"`); volume per device (`mm.reader.device.soundscapeVolume`, default 0.40); "Pause the soundscape during narration" per profile (`pauseSoundscapeForNarration`, default off). `MATCH THE MOOD` (pure `matchTheMood(genres, mood)` in `soundscape-match.ts` + test): case-insensitive, the series' genres in order, the first that appears in: Romance, Josei, Shoujo → Rain on glass; Action, Martial Arts, Murim, Sports → Low drone; Comedy, Slice of Life, School → Café; Horror, Thriller, Mystery, Psychological → Night wind; Fantasy, Isekai, Regression, Cultivation, Historical → Temple bells; Drama, Crime, Sci-Fi → Night city; Adventure, Seinen → Afternoon park; no match → the profile mood's default (default Projector room, romantic Rain on glass, action Low drone, comedy Café, horror Night wind, slice_of_life Afternoon park, fantasy Temple bells).
4. **Lifecycle** (`useHouseSound({ genres, mood })`, used by both readers): with a soundscape set, the loop fades in over 2000 ms when the reader opens (after the first gesture if the context is still locked: the reader's first tap, key or scroll starts it); it fades out over 600 ms on leaving the reader and when the document becomes hidden (and resumes with the 2000 ms fade when visible); under narration (web/15's `isPlaying`) it ducks to 30 %, or pauses when "Pause the soundscape during narration" is on.
5. **Picker rows** (the manga setup sheet's AMBIENT tab, the novel Type sheet's `AMBIENT` group, and web/18's Settings → Ambient, which already has the rows): `OFF`, `MATCH THE MOOD`, then the eight loops as a radio group, each row the name in `type.title`, the one-line description in `type.caption` (Projector room "A soft hum with distant reel ticks.", Rain on glass "Steady rain on a window.", Night city "Distant traffic after dark.", Café "Low voices and cups.", Night wind "Wind across an empty street.", Low drone "A deep, even hum.", Afternoon park "Birds and far-off voices.", Temple bells "Slow bells over a quiet courtyard.") and a `Hear` (`secondary` sm): it pauses the running loop (600 ms fade), plays 5 s of the chosen loop (300 ms fade in, fading out over the last 600 ms), then resumes the running loop (2000 ms fade in); one preview at a time. While fetching, `Hear` shows a 16 px leader dial (after 400 ms); offline and not cached, the row's caption reads "Available when you're online." and `Hear` is disabled. Then the volume slider 0–100 % (§7.20, per device). Point web/18's Settings rows at `soundscape.ts`'s preview and delete `features/reader/soundscape-preview.ts` if nothing else imports it.
6. **Indicator**: a 16 px `waveform` glyph while a loop plays: in the manga running head right after the chapter folio's `caret-down`, inside the title group, with its own 44 px hit (not one of the four trailing buttons), tooltip "House sound: Rain on glass", opening Reading setup at `AMBIENT`; in the novel top bar's title area, opening the Type sheet at its `AMBIENT` group.
7. Haptics: none; UI cues are suppressed while a loop plays (§6).

### D. The guided view (§9.4.3; `screens/reader/GuidedView.tsx`)

1. **Entry**: the setup sheet `LAYOUT` segmented control and Settings → Reading: manga's Layout default (web/18 left both at three values) gain `GUIDED` (`STRIP │ SINGLE │ DOUBLE │ GUIDED`, per series; hidden in read-all); `u` toggles guided view (back to the series' previous layout); the running head's `panel-focus` button, which you insert into web/12's ordered trailing array right after bookmark (web/12 E4), appears once `panels[currentPage]` is a non-empty list, Fill plus the 2 px `spot` rule while guided view is on, tooltip "Guided view".
2. **Presentation**: the viewport shows one panel scaled to fit within 24 px margins (at most 3×) through `setCamera`; the rest of the page dimmed to 15 % by the `color.matte.guided` matte (black at 85 %), drawn as one element at the panel's viewport rect with `box-shadow: 0 0 0 100vmax rgba(0,0,0,0.85)`, moving with the camera; the panel folio `PANEL 3 / 7 · PAGE 18` bottom-left in `type.folio` `ink.100` (on the matte, whose 0.85 black exceeds the 0.60 `ink.100` needs over art); the running head and folio bar behave as in paged mode.
3. **Moving**: tap the right 30 % / swipe left (72 px or 600 px/s) / `→` / `j` → next panel; the left 30 % / swipe right / `←` / `k` → previous; the centre 40 % toggles the chrome (all mirrored for right-to-left series). The camera **dollies** between panels: `setCamera(next, { duration: 0.52, ease: [0.65, 0, 0.35, 1] })` (520 ms `dur.rack`, `ease.turn`), reported as `play("dolly")`; at a page's last panel, next **cuts** to the next page's first panel (`setCamera(…, null)`, haptic `page.turn`, sound `turn` if on); after the chapter's last panel the credits page follows whole. Haptic `page.turn` per panel. Double tap (double-click) shows the whole page: a 320 ms (`dur.column`) zoom out along `ease.turn`, held 1500 ms (`dur.hold.glance`), then back to the panel over 320 ms. Pinch overrides the camera until released (web/12's `pinchZoom`), then the camera dollies back to the current panel. A panel taller than the viewport is walked in `tallPanelSteps` of 75 % of the viewport; with auto-scroll on, the camera pans down it at the auto-scroll speed.
4. **Auto-advance** (option "Guided view auto-advance", default off, per profile; also on whenever auto-scroll is started in guided view): each panel holds, then the camera moves on. `PACE BY WORDS` (the default when the chapter has dialogue text): **1.2 s + 0.25 s per OCR word in the panel, capped at 6 s** (`dur.hold.panel.base` 1200, `.word` 250, `.cap` 6000; a wordless panel 1.2 s, 10 words 3.7 s, 20 words 6 s; `panelHoldMs(words, mode, fixedMs)` in `features/reader/guided.ts` with a test); `FIXED`, or no dialogue text: the fixed hold (2–10 s in 0.5 s steps, default 3.5 s). A 2 px `spot` hold rule under the `PANEL 3 / 7` folio fills over the hold (`ease.linear`). A touch, a key or a manual move pauses auto-advance; `p` or the chip resumes it (the chip reads `▸ AUTO · PACED` or `▸ AUTO · 3.5 S` here). Under reduced motion auto-advance never starts by itself (§14.1) and moves by cuts once started.
5. **Setup rows** (AMBIENT): `Guided view auto-advance` switch, then `PACE BY WORDS │ FIXED` and the fixed-hold stepper 2–10 s (step 0.5, default 3.5), per profile.
6. **States**: analysing the current page: the page shows whole with the folio `PAGE 18 · FINDING PANELS` on the folio-flag ground (§2.1.4: a `#000000` box, 1 px `ink.100` border, 4 × 8 px padding); no panels found or analysis failed: the page shows whole with `PAGE 18 · WHOLE PAGE` and next moves to the next page; offline: works from the saved copy (stored panels, or local analysis); reduced motion: cuts instead of dollies.
7. Semantics and keys: the folio is a polite live region ("Panel 3 of 7, page 18"); `u`, `→`/`j`, `←`/`k`, `p` are registered in the reader's key group and listed in the `?` sheet.

### E. Page-tinted chrome (§9.4.4)

1. **Applied to**: `--page-tint` and `--page-light` (the registered `@property` colours, `syntax: "<color>"`, `inherits: true`) written only on the reader chrome's root (the overlay with the running head, folio bar, ruler and scrims) and on the two desktop gutter elements, never on an ancestor of the strip; the setup sheet sets them on its own root when it opens.
