## Goal

Build the Glass Discover branch on the web client (`frontend/`): **Search** at `/search` (on phones the search orb stretches into the bottom field with the screen above it; on desktop a 56 px glass field with a 220 px filter column), with the idle state (recent, trending, "Browse sources", the Ask card), the scopes All · Library · Sources · Dialogue · Novel text, tiered results whose slow sources **surface from depth**, the tier progress capsule with its liquid fill, the jump bar, and the new on-device **Novel text** index; **Sources** at `/sources` (health line and beads, pins that fly into place, drag to reorder, "Having trouble"); the **Source catalogue** at `/sources/:sourceId` (browse modes as a swipeable pager, the genre chip, the "Opening" lens with orbiting covers, the "Top" capsule, the novel shelf); and **Dialogue search** at `/ocr`, whose results dive into the manga reader, glide to the page and settle the hit lens over the matched bubble. When you finish, the ScreenIds `discover`, `sources`, `source` and `dialogue` leave the Glass `PENDING` map. For you (`/library/recommendations`) is `web/41`. Cinematic must not change by a single pixel.
