Track: web · Order 104 · Depends on: `docs/redesign/prompts/web/42-glass-stats-streak-wrapped.md` · Runs in parallel with: `docs/redesign/prompts/mobile/43-glass-circle.md` · Proof folder: `docs/redesign/proof/web-43/`

## Goal

Deliver new feature 3 of the owner's decisions (social for 2–3 users: see what the other accounts and profiles are reading, reactions on chapters, shared collections, "recommend to" a friend, all respecting per-profile isolation and the 18+ gate) on the Glass web skin, exactly as `glass/DESIGN.md` §9.3 specifies, in the **people light** (`bloom` `#FF9ED8`). Build **Circle** at `/circle` (ScreenId `circle`): the **presence arc** whose orbs drift forward while someone reads (only when that person allowed `show_presence`) and wear shared streaks (`share_streak`), the Activity · Letters · Shelves tabs, collapsed reads and the client-side spoiler guard; **the six named reactions** (Hype, Love, Wrecked, Tears, Twist, Masterpiece; a stored `laughed` rendered as "Laughed") with the hold-to-bloom picker and the thrown glyph, on every surface that shows a chapter; **shared collections** (the share sheet, members' View only and Can add views, adder orbs, Leave shelf); **Recommend to a friend** (letters, the drag-to-orb gesture with the magnet, the recommend sheet, Sent letters); **a friend** at `/circle/:profileId` (ScreenId `circleMember`, a sheet or window through the sheet host); the **settings** (`show_presence`, `share_streak`, Clear my activity, Hide from my Circle); and every **18+ and isolation** state. Every state, desktop, tablet and mobile web, keyboard access and reduced motion. When you finish, the ScreenIds `circle` and `circleMember` leave the Glass `PENDING` set. The server decides who sees what; the client never shows a placeholder, count or "hidden" row for anything it was not sent.
