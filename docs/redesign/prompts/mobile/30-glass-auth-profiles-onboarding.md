## Goal

Build the Glass skin's way in on the Flutter app (iOS and Android, phones and tablets), exactly as `docs/redesign/glass/DESIGN.md` §8.1 to §8.7 specify: **Setup** (the server address, with the Address drain into the lens and the lens opening onto Login); the splash hand-off targets; **Login** with the typed "Welcome back", the server row, the known accounts (`mm.known-accounts`) and the Slab condense and Lens split into the picker; **Register** in its Closed, Bootstrap and Open variants with live validation and every server code on its field; the **profile picker** "Who's reading?" When you finish, `setup`, `login`, `register`, `profiles`, `profileNew`, `profileEdit`, `profilesManage` and `onboarding` leave the Glass `PENDING` set. Glass stays behind the debug row.
