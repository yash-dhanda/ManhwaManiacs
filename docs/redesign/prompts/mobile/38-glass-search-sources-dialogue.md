## Goal

Build the Glass Discover surfaces in the Flutter app: **Search** at `/search` (ScreenId `discover`), opened from the dock's search orb, which stretches into a bottom field that rides on the keyboard while the dock sinks away, with the idle state (Recent, Trending, "Browse sources", the Ask card), the scopes All · Library · Sources · Dialogue · Novel text, tiered results whose slow sources **surface from depth**, the tier progress capsule with its liquid fill, the jump bar, and the new on-device **Novel text** search over downloaded chapters on a SQLite FTS4 table in the existing `sqflite` chapter store; **Sources** at `/sources` (ScreenId `sources`), the root of dock tab 3, with the health line and beads, pins that fly into place, drag to reorder and "Having trouble"; the **Source catalogue** at `/sources/:sourceId` (ScreenId `source`), pushed inside tab 3, with browse modes as a swipeable pager, the genre chip, the "Opening" lens with orbiting covers, the "Top" capsule and the novel shelf; and **Dialogue search** at `/ocr` (ScreenId `dialogue`, alias `/ocr/search`), whose results dive into the Glass manga reader, glide to the page and settle the hit lens over the matched bubble. Every state, hardware key, haptic, reduced-motion variant, tablet and desktop-frame layout is delivered, and every request goes through the `mobile/03` request limiter at its priority. When you finish, `discover`, `sources`, `source` and `dialogue` leave the Glass `PENDING` set. For you (`/library/recommendations`) is `mobile/41`.

AND c.kind = 'novel' WHERE novel_text MATCH ? AND <the §8.0.8 step 5a gate predicate on c's mature stamp from mobile/07, a no-op while the gate is open> ORDER BY ((novel_text.source || ':' || novel_text.series) IN (<readingSeries, at most 500>)) DESC, ((length(offs) - length(replace(offs, ' ', '')) + 1) / 4) DESC, c.read_at IS NULL, c.read_at DESC LIMIT 51`.
