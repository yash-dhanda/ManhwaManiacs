## Goal

Build the Glass frame around every Glass screen in the Flutter app, as `glass/DESIGN.md` §8.0 and §15.3 specify: `glass_skin.dart` (dark `ThemeData`, `BouncingScrollPhysics` everywhere, the Glass `PageTransitionsTheme` for Android); `router.dart` (a `StatefulShellRoute.indexedStack` with the four branches Home, Library, Sources and You; `GlassSwipePage` with `canOnlySwipeFromEdge: false` on iOS and `MaterialPage` with `GlassPageTransitionsBuilder` on Android; `GlassSheetPage` routes for series detail, recap, friend and the profile form, and the `?sheet=` family on any route; a `NavigatorObserver` that tracks depth per branch and captures route snapshots for the stack overview); `shell.dart` with the floating nav row and large title, the **GlassDock** (bar 64, minimised 50, search orb 50) with its droplet, minimise, drag and long-press menus, the bottom accessory, and on tablet and desktop frames the inset sidebar (collapsed 76 px, 280 px on wide desktop frames) with the toolbar row; the stack overview fan on a long-press of Back; the `heroine` 0.7.2 poster zoom and the Dive into readers; back per platform, the Android back order and the takeover rules; the hardware-keyboard key map and the command palette on tablet and desktop frames; the 18+ purge; session loss, profile loss, profile switching and the skin-switch melt; error surfaces and status screens; the global overlays (new chapters, the APK update, What's new); the **Droplet** splash fading in from the black native frame; the haptic and sound maps and the audio-session state machine. Screens stay pending (they render inside this shell), except the `readerLanding` redirect; Glass stays behind the debug row.

## Scope: deliver every item below

Sizes are logical px; hit areas are `GlassFrame.hitMin` (44 iOS, 48 Android); every named move goes through `GlassMotion`; every haptic through `GlassHaptics.fire`; every sound through `skin_audio.dart` with the Glass cue map; every live glass surface is a `SkinGlass` or `SkinGlassGroup` with its `layer`; a bar group (the nav row's leading button, title capsule and trailing group; the dock with its orb and accessory; the toolbar group) is **one** `SkinGlassGroup` layer with one shape per member, and focus rings are painted outside the clip (`GlassFocusRing`). `SkinGlass` is the only importer of `liquid_glass_widgets`: when the shell needs `GlassTabBar`, wrap it in `skins/glass/glass/tab_bar.dart` exported by `skin_glass.dart`. Sections cited are `glass/DESIGN.md`.

### A. Theme, scroll behaviour and the route frame (`glass_skin.dart`, `glass_scroll_behavior.dart`, `routes/route_frame.dart`, §8.0.7, §15.3)

1. `GlassSkin.theme`: `ThemeData(brightness: Brightness.dark, colorScheme: ColorScheme.dark(surface: Color(0xFF000000)), scaffoldBackgroundColor: Color(0xFF000000), pageTransitionsTheme: PageTransitionsTheme(builders: {TargetPlatform.android: GlassPageTransitionsBuilder(), TargetPlatform.iOS: GlassPageTransitionsBuilder()}), textSelectionTheme: TextSelectionThemeData(selectionColor: Color(0x667563F2), cursorColor: <iris400>))`, so every stock widget, the keyboard appearance and the system dialogs are dark.
2. `GlassScrollBehavior extends ScrollBehavior`: `BouncingScrollPhysics(parent: AlwaysScrollableScrollPhysics())` on every platform, no glow and no stretch indicator, `GlassScrollbar` (`mobile/27`) as the scrollbar; installed with `ScrollConfiguration` at the Glass root. Every Glass scroll view uses `keyboardDismissBehavior: ScrollViewKeyboardDismissBehavior.onDrag` (a lint-free helper `glassScrollDefaults` the screens pass).
3. **System UI:** status and navigation bars transparent with light icons (`SystemChrome.setSystemUIOverlayStyle`), edge-to-edge; iOS 120 Hz stays on (`CADisableMinimumFrameDurationOnPhone`, verify `Info.plist` and report if missing); `android:enableOnBackInvokedCallback="true"` (verify, `mobile/01` set it).
4. **Route frame:** every page the router builds is wrapped in `GlassRouteFrame(routeKey:, child:)`: a `RepaintBoundary` with a `GlobalKey` (the stack-overview capture target), a `BackdropGroup` (one per route, required by the frost path of `mobile/25` G4), and a `Focus` scope for route focus (item K3).

### B. Frames, insets and focus scrolling (`shell/insets.dart`, `shell/focus_policy.dart`, §2.2, §8.0.1)

1. Frames come from `GlassFrame.of(context)` (`mobile/25`): phone (shorter side < 600), tablet (≥ 600 and width < 1024), desktop (width ≥ 1024), wide (≥ 1440). Phones stay portrait through `GlassOrientationScope` (`mobile/25`); tablets rotate and change frame with their width.
2. `GlassInsets.of(context)` → `{top, bottom}` for scroll content: phone top `safeTop + 60`, bottom `safeBottom + 85` (+ 56 while the accessory shows); tablet and desktop frames top 76 (`toolbarTop` 12 + `toolbarHeight` 48 + 16), bottom 24; while the keyboard is open (`MediaQuery.viewInsetsOf(context).bottom > 0`) the bottom inset is `safeBottom + 16`. Content columns: margins from `GlassFrame.screenMargin`; beside the sidebar content starts at 304 (expanded) or 100 (collapsed), max width 1440.
3. `GlassFocusTraversalPolicy` (a `ReadingOrderTraversalPolicy` subclass) with a `requestFocusCallback` that, after `Scrollable.ensureVisible`, checks whether the focused rect intersects the top band (safe-top + 60 on phones, 76 on wider frames) or the bottom band (safe-bottom + 85, + 56 with the accessory, 24 on wider frames) and animates the nearest `ScrollPosition` by the overlap plus 8 px on `springSettle`, so hardware-keyboard focus never lands under a bar (§2.2, WCAG 2.4.11). Installed at the Glass root.

### C. Top bars (`shell/glass_scaffold.dart`, `nav_row.dart`, `large_title.dart`, `toolbar.dart`, `glass_back_button.dart`, `edges.dart`, §7.14, §7.32, §7.37)

Screens never build their own top bar.
2. **Nav row (phone and tablet frames):** 44 tall at `safeTop + 8`, inset by the screen margin (tablet: over the content column). Leading: `GlassBackButton` on pushed screens, or the profile orb (`ProfileOrb` 44 wearing `GoalRing` from `glassGoalRingProvider`, item K8; tap → `/more`; long-press 450 ms → the profile switcher `GlassMenu`, item L4). Centre: the title capsule (`glassThin`, 36 tall, `subhead` 15/20 at `wght` 600 `onGlass`, max 60 % of the row, ellipsis) once the large title has scrolled under the row; while offline or syncing, `GlassStatusCapsule` takes the capsule's place ("Offline" with `wifi-slash` on the backing disc; "Syncing" with a spinner while the first refetch burst after reconnection runs, within 10 s of it). Trailing: a `GlassGroup` of up to three icons; `contentModeSwitch: true` adds `GlassContentModeSwitch(variant: navRow)` before them. The row is one `SkinGlassGroup` (leading, centre, trailing: up to 3 shapes); its shape list is rebuilt at rest, never mid-motion.
3. **`GlassLargeTitle`:** `largeTitle` (34/40 phone, 36/42 tablet, 40/46 desktop, 44/50 wide, `wght` 700, tracking −0.020 em), 16 px below the nav row, `Semantics(header: true)` with a `FocusNode` (route focus target), rendered through `LetterReveal` once per session per screen (`revealKey "{profileId}:{screenId}:title"`). Built as a `SliverPersistentHeader` whose delegate interpolates the title's position and size from the scroll offset into the capsule (Title capsule: the capsule materialises, scale 0.9 → 1 on `springSnappy`); scrolling back reverses it. Pulling past the top (negative offset under `BouncingScrollPhysics`, or `mobile/27`'s pull-to-refresh extent) stretches the title up to 1.08 anchored at its leading edge. ### D. Dock, search orb and bottom accessory (`shell/dock.dart`, `dock_geometry.dart`, `dock_droplet.dart`, `search_orb.dart`, `accessory.dart`, `accessory_controller.dart`, `dock_menus.dart`, `glass/tab_bar.dart`, §7.15)

Everything below applies to both.
2. **Geometry (`dock_geometry.dart`, pure, tested):** a `glassRegular` (T3) capsule 64 tall, 21 px from the left and bottom (+ safe-bottom), its right edge leaving room for the 50 px orb and an 8 px gap before the 21 px inset. `GlassDockGeometry.of(Size size, EdgeInsets padding)` exposes the dock, orb, accessory and minimised rects and `tabRect(GlassTab tab)` (`mobile/30` flies the chosen orb and the onboarding droplet to them before the shell mounts). Tabs with equal widths: Home (`house-simple`, `/`), Library (`books`, `/library`), Sources (`globe-hemisphere-west`, `/sources`), You (the profile orb 24 px centred on the 22 px icon slot, wearing the goal ring; `/more`). Each tab: icon 22 over `tabLabel` 11/13 `wght` 600; the active glyph `iris400` (Duotone, morphing to Fill on press; no backing disc on the plateau, §2.1.2), the active label `onGlass` at `wght` 700.
3. **Droplet:** a `glassFilm` clear capsule 56 × 52 under the active tab. Tapping another tab moves it on `springTab` (k 195.0, c 21.78) with the stretch `scaleX = 1 + min(|v| / 2000, 0.25)`, `scaleY = 1 / sqrt(scaleX)`, and fires `nav.change`. Dragging across the dock (press on the droplet or anywhere on the dock and slide): the droplet lifts (fully clear, +6 %), follows the finger on `springTrack`, fires `nav.scrub` over each tab, rubber-bands at most 18 px past the first and last tab (`rubberband`), and on release projects (`project()`) to the nearest tab and settles on `springTab`; a touch catches it in flight (`catchMotion()`, `motion.catch`). The droplet takes the field's rim tint at 12 % (Light follows the story).
4. **Merge:** dragging the droplet past the last tab toward the orb bridges the two with a metaball neck when the gap is under 12 px (a `CustomPainter` path added to the group's mask only; no extra layer); releasing on the orb opens search (Dock merge).
5. **Minimise:** after 20 px of cumulative downward scroll (`thresholdDockHide`, from the nearest primary `ScrollNotification`) the dock morphs on `springMinimize` (k 246.7, c 31.42) into a 50 px capsule showing only the active tab's icon; the orb stays; 12 px of upward scroll (`thresholdDockShow`), reaching the top of a list, a tap on the capsule, or focus on one of its tab nodes restores it. Reversing the scroll mid-morph reverses it with its current velocity. It never minimises while `glassAssistiveProvider` is true (§14.5). Minimised, all four tab `Semantics` nodes stay in the tree.
6. **Badges:** Home an 8 px `iris400` dot while the unread update count is above 0; Library a count badge (`mobile/26` `count`, "9+") of queued + downloading + failed chapters from the shared download queue; You a `bloom` dot while `lettersProvider` has a letter in state `newLetter` (hidden when the Circle answers 404).
7. **Tap the active tab:** pop the branch to its root (`navigationShell.goBranch(i, initialLocation: true)`), or at the root scroll to top on `springPage` (the branch's primary `ScrollController` through `PrimaryScrollController`); `nav.reselect`, and `nav.root` once per level popped (40 ms apart, at most 4).
8. **Long-press a tab** (450 ms): a `GlassMenu` blooming from the tab with a non-interactive header row naming the tab in `headline`. `dock_menus.dart` holds `registerDockMenu(GlassTab tab, List<GlassMenuEntry> Function(WidgetRef ref) build)` (returns a disposer): Library → Shelf, Collections, History, Bookmarks, Downloads and the first five collections; Sources → the pinned sources (`sourcePinsProvider`); You → the profile switcher (each other profile as a 32 px orb row; choosing one runs L4 without visiting the picker); Home → the header row and "Updates" until `mobile/31` registers its items.
9. **Hidden** in both readers, on bare and takeover routes, while a sheet sits at `large` (`GlassRecedeScope.sheetProgress == 1`; sheet routes live on the root navigator, above the shell, item G3), and while the keyboard is open: when `MediaQuery.viewInsetsOf(context).bottom > 0`, the dock, orb and accessory leave on `springMinimize`, translating down by their height + safe-bottom, and return on `springMinimize` when it closes.
10. **Semantics:** the dock is `Semantics(container: true, explicitChildNodes: true, label: "Main")`; each tab `Semantics(selected:, button: true, label: "Library, tab 2 of 4")`; hardware keyboard `←`/`→` move focus along the tabs and `Enter`/`Space` select. The drag is a pointer-only enhancement and ends exactly like a tap. The orb is a separate button "Search".
11. **Search orb:** a 50 px `glassThin` circle on the dock's bottom line. A tap pushes `/search`, a root-navigator `GoRoute` whose page is a `CustomTransitionPage` (`transitionDuration` 434 ms, the `morph` settle): the orb stretches along `springMorph` into `mobile/26`'s `GlassSearchField(variant: bottom)` (50 tall `glassRegular`, full width minus 21 px insets, riding the keyboard) while the dock sinks on `springMinimize`; above the field the page renders the `discover` screen (pending until `mobile/38`, which builds its body into this slot). (401) or "This account was deactivated. Ask the server's owner." (`account_disabled`), one tinted button "Sign in" ("OK" for `account_disabled`); `warning` once. "Sign in" → `/login?user={username}` (the cached username, never the password; `mobile/30` fills it and focuses the password). The remembered profile stays. The alert paints in Glass because the device mirror is Glass.
2. **Profile gone** (`profile_required`, `profile_not_found`): the toast "That profile is no longer available" (`warning`), playback stops, and the app cross-fades to `/profiles` over 200 ms (no Step into the light, no Droplet).
3. **`prepareProfileSwitch(Ref ref, Profile target)`** (used by L4 and by `mobile/30`'s picker): stop narration, cruise and the soundscape (no Undo); pause the current profile's download queue (the new profile's queue resumes after the switch); cancel in-flight recaps and their toasts (`mobile/41` registers a canceller); run the purge first when the target's gate is closed; set the active profile; decide the skin with the boot resolver (`profile.skin ?? default` against the running Glass skin, honouring the debug preference and `Flags.glassAvailable`); return `{SkinId? restartTo}`; `commitProfileSwitch(ref, destination)` increments `glassRouterEpochProvider` with the destination (`Routes.tonight` or the onboarding step), which resets every branch.
4. **Short hand-off** (the dock's You long-press, the sidebar's account menu): the chosen orb flies from its menu row to the dock's You tab (phone) or the sidebar's profile capsule (tablet and desktop) on `springZoom` in `GlassHandoffLayer` (an overlay installed in the app `builder`, above the router, so it survives the router reset), the field pours to the new profile's mood over 600 ms (`curveTintShift`), `profile.select`, then `commitProfileSwitch(ref, Routes.tonight)`; with `restartTo` set, the melt (L5) plays instead of the flight and the restart follows (`switchSkin`-free: the profile already carries its skin, so write the mirror and the return route `/` and call `AppRestart.of(context).restart()`), with no alert and no Undo.
5. **`playMelt()`** (the Skin melt, 615 ms, reused by `mobile/30` and `mobile/39`): every live `SkinGlass` dematerialises (the registry's `dematerializeAll()`, 350 ms), the shell root blurs 0 → 40 px on `springSmooth` (`ImageFiltered`), and a circular `ClipPath` closes from the screen diagonal to the centre on `springPage` over `#000000`; `skin.switch` haptic, the `melt` cue; reduced motion: a 200 ms fade to black. `unmelt()` reverses it over 200 ms.
6. **`startSkinSwitch(BuildContext context, {required Rect sourceRect})`** (the palette's Skin action now; `mobile/39` adds the Settings cards on the same function): the §8.25.2 alert blooming from `sourceRect`: title "Restart in Cinematic?", body "Everything about the app changes: layout, navigation, type and motion. Your library, progress and downloads stay exactly as they are, and you'll come back to this screen.", the inline notice "Downloads pause for a moment and resume after the restart." when the queue is not empty, and when offline the line "This profile will switch on your other devices once you're back online."; buttons "Stay in Glass" (secondary, first, initial focus) and "Restart in Cinematic" (the alert's tinted action). Confirm → `switchSkin(...)` (`mobile/01`; the PATCH goes through the offline outbox) with `playMelt` as the outgoing animation; a failure shows the toast "Couldn't switch skins. Try again.".
7. **Arriving into Glass:** the splash reads `mm.skin.t0` before `mobile/01`'s restart logger clears it; when present (an explicit switch), the toast "Switched to Glass" with "Undo" and a 10 s draining rim shows after the Droplet; Undo runs `switchSkin` back with no alert.
8. **Boot-time mismatch** (§8.2): when the remembered profile's saved skin differs from the mirror, the Droplet plays to the lens and the restart runs from the lens with no confirm: if `mobile/01`'s boot restarts before the splash runs, add a skin-neutral `Future<void> Function()? beforeBootRestart` to the `Skin` interface (Cinematic returns `null`; one line under `skins/cinematic/`), awaited by the boot; Glass's waits for the splash lens to settle and plays `playMelt()`.
9. **`arrival.dart`:** `glassArrivalProvider` (`StateProvider<GlassArrival?>`, `{kind: profile | onboarding, Offset point}`); on mount the shell reads and clears it and materialises the dock (a circular clip growing from the point on `springZoom`) or the sidebar capsule from that point; `mobile/30` writes it before navigating.
