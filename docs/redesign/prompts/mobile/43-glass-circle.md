# Mobile Glass Circle (new feature 3)

Track: mobile · Order 105 · Depends on: `docs/redesign/prompts/mobile/42-glass-stats-streak-wrapped.md` · Web twin: `docs/redesign/prompts/web/43-glass-circle.md` (runs in parallel) · Proof folder: `docs/redesign/proof/mobile-43/`

## Goal

Deliver new feature 3 of the owner's decisions (social for 2–3 users: see what the other accounts and profiles are reading, reactions on chapters, shared collections, "recommend to" a friend, all respecting per-profile isolation and the 18+ gate) on the Glass skin of the Flutter app, exactly as `glass/DESIGN.md` §9.3 specifies, in the **people light** (`bloom` `#FF9ED8`). Build **Circle** at `/circle` (ScreenId `circle`): the **presence arc** whose orbs drift forward while someone reads (only when that person allowed `show_presence`) and wear shared streaks (`share_streak`), the Activity · Letters · Shelves tabs, collapsed reads and the spoiler guard; **the six named reactions** (Hype, Love, Wrecked, Tears, Twist, Masterpiece; a stored `laughed` rendered as "Laughed") with the hold-to-bloom picker, the `reaction.bloom`, `reaction.cross` and `reaction.send` haptics and the thrown glyph, on every surface that shows a chapter; **shared collections** (the share sheet, members' View only and Can add views, adder orbs, Leave shelf); **Recommend to a friend** (letters, the drag-to-orb gesture on a lifted poster with the magnet, the recommend sheet, Sent letters); **a friend** at `/circle/:profileId` (ScreenId `circleMember`) as a `GlassSheetPage` sheet route with the orb flying into its header; the **settings** (`show_presence`, `share_streak`, Clear my activity, Hide from my Circle); and every **18+ and isolation** state. Every state, phone, tablet and desktop frames, hardware keyboards, VoiceOver and TalkBack, and reduced motion. When you finish, the ScreenIds `circle` and `circleMember` leave the Glass `PENDING` map. The server decides who sees what; the app never shows a placeholder, count or "hidden" row for anything it was not sent. Do not touch `frontend/`.
