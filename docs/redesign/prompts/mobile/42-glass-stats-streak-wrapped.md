## Goal

Deliver new feature 2 of the owner's decisions (reading stats and streaks: a Wrapped-style yearly recap, chapters per day, time read, genre radar, the streak flame, shareable stat cards with image export) on the Glass skin of the Flutter app, exactly as `glass/DESIGN.md` §9.2 specifies. Build **"Your reading"** at `/library/statistics` (ScreenId `numbers`): the hero with the physics **streak flame** and the **daily goal ring** fed by `today_seconds`, the totals, every chart as a `CustomPainter` with its summary sentence, drag-to-scrub readout, semantics actions and "Show as table", the ranges `7 d · 30 d · 90 d · Year` remembered in `mm.stats.range`, the Wrapped entry card and the daily-goal menu; **the streak flame** everywhere it appears, its tip leaning with device tilt (`sensors_plus` 7.1.0, through the one shared gravity source that the onboarding genre field also uses, sampled at 30 Hz only while a consumer is visible) and with scroll acceleration, the **flare** driven only by the `POST /reader/progress` answer (no local inference), the embers, the **record sparks** and the once-per-profile **milestones**; **Wrapped** at `/library/statistics/annual/:year` (ScreenId `annual`): the takeover story of up to 12 cards in one 360 × 640 frame, with the reflowing large-text column at text scale 1.3 and above, the count-up, the page pile, the podium, busiest day, firsts and lasts, the top source lens, Together and the summary; and **share cards** in `mobile/lib/skins/glass/wrapped/share_card.dart` (lift and flip to the share side, Story 1080 × 1920 and Post 1080 × 1350 PNGs rendered through `RepaintBoundary.toImage(pixelRatio: 3)` and shared with `share_plus` 12.0.2, drawn from the server's `shareable` blocks only, "Save image" to Photos or `Pictures/ManhwaManiacs`). Every state, phone, tablet and desktop frames, hardware keyboards, VoiceOver and TalkBack, and reduced motion. When you finish, the ScreenIds `numbers` and `annual` leave the Glass `PENDING` map. No chart, carousel or physics package. Do not touch `frontend/`.
