# Mobile Glass manga reader

Track: mobile · Order 89 · Depends on: `docs/redesign/prompts/mobile/34-reader-engine-glass-commands.md` · Web twin: `docs/redesign/prompts/web/35-glass-manga-reader.md` · Proof folder: `docs/redesign/proof/mobile-35/`

## Goal

Build the Glass manga reader in the Flutter app (`mobile/`, iOS and Android), glass §8.14, as chrome over the shared reader engine (`ReaderEngineView` and its `chromeBuilder(context, ReaderEngineState)` slot in `mobile/lib/features/reader/engine/`): the black canvas with brightness, warmth and colour layers; floating `glassRegular` capsules tinted by the current page with the adaptive legibility dim; the scrub rail with its magnifier lens and the go-to-page popover; auto-hide and the minimised pill, cinema and locked modes; the gestures (pinch, anchored double tap, tap bands, the pull past the chapter end that arms at 48 and commits at 72 with momentum, the opt-in sideways chapter swipe); the phone-only **left-edge brightness band** (12 % of the width, from x 24 px on iOS); chapter seams and the seam chip; paged single and double with Slide, Fade and None; read-all; the reader settings sheet with the migrated values of §8.25.3 (including the app-only rows Keep screen awake, Refresh rate and Volume keys); every state and hardware-keyboard key; the in-reader chapter list; the dialogue overlay with on-device Extract text and the 1.12 × hit lens; the reader system UI (`immersiveSticky`, stable insets on Android through the new `mm/platform` method `display.stableInsets`); `gestures.setExclusionRects` for the scrub rail and the band's middle 200 dp on Android; orientations widened on enter and restored on exit; the landscape corner capsules; volume keys; the desktop-frame side panels and page-lit gutters on large tablets; and the manga-reader row of the live-surface budget with its **8-member `BackdropGroup` budget** on the frost path. The skin never moves the page layer itself: every page movement is an engine command. When you finish, the ScreenIds `reader` and `readAll` leave the Glass `PENDING` map (`readerLanding` left it in `mobile/29`, which registered the `/reader` → `/library` redirect; this step only verifies it), and both entry points (the library reader, mobile S15, and the source reader, mobile S19) are proven. Glass stays behind Settings → Diagnostics → Edition (debug); Cinematic must not change by a single pixel.

Chrome show and hide never fire a haptic.
8. **Cinema mode** (`c`, or the settings sheet): the pill hides too after 3 s idle; a 2 px micro-progress line stays at the very bottom edge unless "Hide progress" (`glass.hideCinemaProgress`) is on.
9. **Cruise button:** toggles cruise through `startAutoScroll(cruiseSpeed × 60)` and stop, `p` toggles, `<` / `>` step 0.25× within 0.25–4 (written to `cruiseSpeed`), `autoscroll.toggle`; a tap or a manual scroll pauses it; it never starts on its own under reduced motion. The flywheel pill, its drag, the HUD, the 400 ms ramp and flick to cruise are `mobile/44`.
10. **Keep screen awake** (the one rule of §8.14.5, through the existing `reader_wakelock.dart`): `WakelockPlus.enable()` while the reader is in the foreground and `glass.keepAwake` is on; always on while cruise runs, whatever the switch says; `disable()` on leaving the reader and on `AppLifecycleState.paused`; with the switch off, released 2 s after cruise stops. Narration never holds it.
11. **Refresh rate and volume keys** (Android): the K04 pin through the existing `reader_display_mode.dart` while the reader is mounted, reset on exit; volume keys through `NativeBridge.setVolumeKeyNavEnabled(true)` only while the reader is mounted, in the foreground and K08 is on (false on dispose and on `paused`): volume down is the next page and volume up the previous (strip: `pageBy`; paged: `turnTo` with `page.turn`).
12. **Locked mode** (`glass.lockControls`): taps only scroll; five taps in the centre region (20–80 % × 15–85 %) within 2 s unlock (a small lock glyph pulses at each tap; toast "Reader unlocked"; `reader.unlock`); with a screen reader running or a hardware keyboard attached, an "Unlock controls" `Semantics` custom action on the page and `u` unlock at once.
13. **Tap light:** a 120 px radial light at 10 % white blooms at the tap point and fades over 300 ms (opacity only, also under reduced motion).
14. **Zoom chip:** a `glassThin` capsule top-centre showing "200 %" for 1,200 ms after a pinch or a trackpad zoom.
15. **Toasts in the reader:** top-centre, 60 px below `inset.top` (under the top chrome band), never bottom.

### E. Page-tinted chrome and the legibility dim (§9.4.4, §2.1.7; `screens/reader/page_tint_chrome.dart` + test)

1. **Source:** `state.currentPageSample` (manifest tint first, then the decoded sample; the engine holds updates above 3,000 px/s and applies the greyscale rules).
2. **Tint:** the sample's `tint` clamped in OKLCH (`core/color/oklch.dart`) to L 0.35–0.50 and C ≤ 0.12; applied as a tint layer inside every reader glass surface at 18 %, to the rims at 22 % (the rim tint at L 0.86, C ≤ 0.08), to the soft edges at 30 %, to the minimised pill's inner glow at 30 %, and to the micro-progress line. Changes cross-fade over `curveTintShift` (900 ms, `Cubic(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`) through one `TweenAnimationBuilder<Color?>` per chrome root, and apply only when the new tint differs from the current by ΔE (OKLab) > 0.04. If `SkinGlass` has no tint input, add `Color? tint` (the 18 % layer) and `Color? rimTint` inside `skin_glass.dart` with a test, leaving every other caller unchanged. Pure helpers `clampTint(Color)`, `rimTint(Color)`, `deltaE(Color, Color)`; tests for a saturated red, a near-grey and the 0.04 gate.
3. **Legibility:** `lb` per surface: the top groups use `pTop`; the bottom capsule, the pill and the micro-progress use `pBottom`; every other reader surface (popover, page menu, next-chapter card, seam chip, zoom chip, loading capsule, match capsule, band HUD, the settings sheet) uses the maximum of `pTop`, `pMid` and `pBottom` over the viewport bands its rect overlaps (top quarter, middle half, bottom quarter). `SkinGlass` sets `dim = clamp(0.22 + 0.42 × lb, 0.22, 0.64)` and the label `GRAD` over `curveDimShift` (400 ms); with no sample yet `lb = 1.0` (dim 0.64). Pure `bandLb(Rect surface, Size viewport, PageSample? s)` + test.
4. **Switch** (settings → Ambient → Page-tinted chrome, default on). Off: neutral glass; the dim still adapts. Solid glass or iOS Reduce Transparency: the tint stays on the solid chrome's rim only.
5. **Desktop frames:** the side panels' rims take the same tint; the page-lit gutters are §K2.

### F. Strip, seams, boundaries and paged (§8.14.1, §8.14.4)

1. **Continuous (default):** the engine stitches neighbours with seams. The seam (`bandBuilder` `seam`, 96 px, a content twin): a hairline, "CHAPTER 144" `caption1` +0.2 em with `LetterReveal` when the seam enters (`seamProgress` turns non-null), the chapter title `footnote` `label2`, a hairline. On `SeamEvent.top` a `glassThin` chip "Chapter 144" sticks under the top group for 1,200 ms (Seam chip: materialise, hold, dematerialise); on `SeamEvent.readingLine` fire `chapter.seam`.
2. **Missing chapters:** when chapter numbers jump (143 → 145), the seam shows a `warning` row "Chapter 144 is missing from this source".
3. **Neighbour still loading** (`nextLoading`): the tail seam shows a 16 px liquid ring spinner and "Chapter 144 is on its way" (`footnote` `label2`); the head seam the same for the previous chapter. **Failed neighbour** (`nextFailed`): the seam card "Chapter 144 didn't load" + "Try again" + "Open it on its own", with the engine's back-off 2, 4, 8, 16, 30 s (cap 30 s).
4. **Read-all:** the strip streams the whole series in order (chapter 1 or `?from=` first); seams are slim (48 px) with no card and no pause.
5. **One at a time** (`glass.chapters = SINGLE`, engine `chapterMode: single`): past the last page the strip rubber-bands (the engine's `ChapterEndPhysics`); on `NeighbourEvent(armed)` call `armNeighbour(next)` and raise the `glassThick` **next-chapter card** from the bottom like a partial sheet, its position driven every frame by `engine.live.overscrollExtent` (a `ValueListenableBuilder`): the next chapter's first page as a thumbnail tilted 8° in depth (`Matrix4.identity()..setEntry(3, 2, 1 / 900)..rotateX(8°)`, following device tilt ±4° through the Glass tilt provider), "Chapter 144", "42 pages · about 6 min" from `NeighbourInfo`, and a tinted "Read next" twin; `chapter.arm`. At 72 px (`locked`) the card locks with `chapter.next`; releasing zooms the card to full screen on `springZoom` (k 125.9, c 21.09) carrying the release velocity, then `commitNeighbour(next, velocity:)` and `continueFling(velocity)`. Pulling down past the first page does the same with the previous chapter's card from the top. A `locked` event with `via: wheel` (a trackpad or mouse on a tablet) commits at once with zero velocity. "Read next" and `l` commit without a pull. Reduced motion: the card appears at the lock and the switch is a 200 ms cross-fade with no carried momentum.
6. **Auto next** (`autoNextChapter`, default on): in one-at-a-time mode, reaching the end with the chrome hidden commits the next chapter after 900 ms unless the reader scrolls back.
7. **Caught up** (the last published chapter): the end card "You're caught up" / "The source hasn't published chapter 145 yet." and, when the series is not in the library, "Add to library to hear about new chapters" with an "Add to library" button (`POST /library/follow`, `follow.add`).
8. **Sideways chapter swipe** (opt-in `glass.swipeChapter`, strip only): `lockAxis` decides; while locked horizontally, `swipeNeighbour(dx)` drives a content-twin title card of the neighbour chapter sliding in from the side by `displayed`; release calls `releaseSwipeNeighbour(dx, vx)`; a commit fires `chapter.next`. The drag never starts inside the brightness band or the scrub rail.
9. **Paged** (`SINGLE` or `DOUBLE`; a series with no stored layout switched to paged on a desktop frame or a landscape tablet selects Double, otherwise Single): fit width, height or original; right-to-left mirrors spreads; 8 px spread gap; page turns per `glass.pageTransition`: **Slide** (finger-tracked in the engine's `PageView` with `GlassPagePhysics`, projection decides, settling on `springPage` k 146.0, c 24.17, the incoming page sliding over with a `0 0 8 rgba(0,0,0,0.45)` shadow on its moving edge whose alpha scales from 0 to 0.45 with turn progress, drawn through `pageOverlayBuilder`), **Fade** (160 ms), **None**; `page.turn` on every committed turn. Tap bands 30 / 40 / 30: left previous, centre chrome, right next, mirrored for right-to-left until the reader sets their own (`tapZones`). Zoomed pages (above 1.0) pan and never turn.
10. **Broken page** (`pageStateBuilder` `broken`, inside the page box): a 72 px lens drawn as a content twin with the `image-broken` glyph, "This page didn't load", and a "Retry" button in the same content style that reloads only that image.
11. **Saving the next chapter** (phones, the shared engine duty): verify, with the existing `NextChapterAutoQueue` test extended to both Glass entry points, that opening a manga chapter whose next chapter is not saved queues that one chapter once per open, skipped under Wi-Fi only off Wi-Fi, with "Save the next chapter while I read" off, or at the cap or the 1.5 GB floor; no toast, no haptic.

### G. Gestures and input (§8.14.3, §11; `screens/reader/reader_gestures.dart` + test)

2. **Tap (strip):** toggles the chrome anywhere (default). 8. **Who owns a drag:** the brightness band (C5), the scrub rail (D6) and the sideways swipe (F8) never fight: a drag starting inside the band or the rail's hit strip belongs to it; the sideways swipe starts only outside both.
