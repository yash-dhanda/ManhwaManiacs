# Mobile Glass novel reader

Track: mobile · Order 91 · Depends on: `docs/redesign/prompts/mobile/35-glass-manga-reader.md` · Web twin: `docs/redesign/prompts/web/36-glass-novel-reader.md` (may run at the same time in another session; you never touch `frontend/`) · Proof folder: `docs/redesign/proof/mobile-36/`

## Goal

Build the Glass novel reader in the Flutter app (`mobile/`, iOS and Android, phone, tablet and the Flutter desktop frame): a text column laid on one of seven dark papers (Void, Ink, Night Paper, Dusk, Moss, Rosewood and the book's own "Glass" paper) with floating glass capsules tinted by the paper's ink; the chapter header, the body in Literata, Sans or Atkinson with speaker bands, the end matter and the seamless next chapter pulled up in place; scroll reading and paged reading, where paged mode shows the skin-neutral engine command `paginateNovel` (pre-laid-out `TextPainter` pages) in a `PageView` with the three Glass page turns Slide, Lift and Fade; native text selection (the platform's handles and magnifier through `SelectionArea`) with the Glass selection menu (Copy, Bookmark this paragraph, Play from here, React to this chapter, Recommend to…); the Aa "Type and page" sheet with the paper orbs and the paper ripple; the Contents sheet; pinch for text size; every state and every hardware key; side panels on the desktop frame. When you finish, the ScreenId `novel` leaves the Glass `PENDING` set. Listen mode (the listen row, the full player, the voices) is `mobile/37`; recaps, Circle and the ambient extras (cruise, soundscape, the Aa badge) are `mobile/41`, `mobile/43` and `mobile/44`. Cinematic and its tests must not change behaviour.

D2. **Desktop-frame panels** (Flutter shortest side ≥ 600 and width ≥ 1024, §8.0.1): content-layer surfaces filled with `oklabMix(paper.bg, Color(0xFF131317), 0.30)` (paper 70 %, `#131317` 30 %) at `materialThick` alpha 0.84, radius 26, inset 12, never Liquid Glass; no `BackdropFilter`, because a panel never covers text and the paper beneath it is flat (name this in the report). **Left panel 300 wide, Contents** (`t`): the contents list of item I with the current chapter centred. **Right panel 360 wide**, tabs **Aa · Voices · Listen** as `mobile/27`'s in-page tab strip built from an ordered list; this step delivers only **Aa** (item H inline); `mobile/37` appends Voices and Listen. `,` opens Aa. ### F. Touching the text (§8.15.3)

F1. **Native selection:** the scroll column (and, in paged mode, each page) sits inside one `SelectionArea` with `magnifierConfiguration: TextMagnifier.adaptiveMagnifierConfiguration`, so the platform's long-press, handles and magnifier start and drive selection (Flutter's `SelectionArea` long-press is `kLongPressTimeout`, 500 ms; §8.15.3 says 450 ms; keep the platform's and record the difference in the report). A `Listener` beside it fires `press.lift` (`soft(0.4)`) 150 ms after a pointer lands without moving more than 18 px, cancelled on move or up. The `contextMenuBuilder` returns `GlassSelectionMenu` (`glassThick` T4, min width 220, rows 44, radius 20 inside 26, blooming above `selectableRegionState.contextMenuAnchors` on `springMorph`, `longpress.open` the first time it builds for a selection) with **Copy**, **Bookmark this paragraph**, **Play from here**, **React to this chapter**, **Recommend to…**; it rebuilds with the new anchors while the handles move; a tap outside clears the selection (`SelectableRegionState.clearSelection()`) and closes it; Android back clears it first (B4).
F2. **Which paragraph:** the menu resolves "the paragraph under the press" as the first visible paragraph whose `RenderParagraph.selections` is not empty (paragraph `GlobalKey`s registered by index).
F3. **Copy:** `Clipboard.setData(ClipboardData(text: selectedText))` (the `SelectedContent` from `SelectionArea.onSelectionChanged`); toast "Copied".
F4. **Bookmark this paragraph:** anchor, fraction 0 and a 180-character snippet through the controller's `bookmark()` and the bookmark outbox (`media_type: "novel"`); `bookmark.add` (`success`) and the `add` sound; toast "Saved this spot · Add note" in the paper palette ("Add note" opens a one-line note sheet `?sheet=note` that saves through the same upsert); failure toast "Couldn't save that spot".
F5. **Play from here:** rendered only when the chapter's audio exists. It calls `playFrom(paragraphIndex)` on a `GlassListenBridge` (an `InheritedWidget` plus a Riverpod `Provider` in `mobile/lib/skins/glass/screens/novel/listen_bridge.dart`) whose default implementation seeks `mobile/15`'s `NarrationController` to the first segment of that paragraph and plays; `p` toggles play and pause through `toggle()`. `mobile/37` replaces the default with the full Glass narration host; keep the shape `{available, playing, playFrom(int para), toggle()}`.
F6. **React to this chapter:** the row morphs into `mobile/28`'s `ReactionPicker` at the same anchor (six bubbles: Hype `fire`, Love `heart`, Wrecked `smiley-melting`, Tears `drop`, Twist `lightning`, Masterpiece `hands-clapping`; `reaction.bloom`, `reaction.cross` per bubble); choosing one sends `POST /circle/reactions {source_id, series_key, chapter_key, kind}` through the shared reactions mutation with the kinds `hype`, `loved`, `wrecked`, `tears`, `shook`, `chefs_kiss`; the glyph flies on a ballistic arc (gravity 2,400 px/s², `physicsGravityReaction`) to the end-matter reaction strip when it is mounted and on screen, otherwise to the title capsule, and lands with a `tick` pop and a 6-particle `bloom` burst (`reaction.send` = `ahap:pop`, the `pop` sound). Failure: the glyph falls back with the Error shake and the toast "Couldn't send your reaction"; offline: queued by the shared mutation and drawn at once; sharing off: the send still works and the toast adds "Only you see this. Turn on Circle sharing to show others." Reduced motion: no arc, the glyph appears at its target with a 150 ms fade.
F7. **Recommend to…:** pushes `?sheet=recommend&series={sourceId}:{seriesKey}`. Render the row only when the Glass `?sheet=` registry of `mobile/29` has a `recommend` entry (`mobile/43` registers it); until then the row is absent. When present and nobody accepts recommendations, the row reads "Recommend to… (no one is taking recommendations yet)" and is disabled with that reason (§9.3.4).
F8. **Double tap** does nothing (single taps are never delayed). Hardware keys: `b` bookmarks the paragraph at the reading line; Shift + arrows extend the selection through `SelectableRegion`'s keyboard intents; `Shift+F10` with a selection calls `showToolbar()`.

### G. Reading modes and page turns (§8.15.4)

G1. **Scroll** (default): a `ListView.builder` of paragraphs with `GlassChapterEndPhysics`; the reading line is 38 % from the top; progress is the paragraph bucket at the reading line.
G2. **Paged:** `paginateNovel` pages in a `PageView.builder` (page height = viewport height − the top and bottom reserved bands, which are the chrome heights plus 16 px each); re-paginates on size changes, every Type change and after the fonts load, keeping the first paragraph of the current page on screen. The progress bucket is `round(pageIndex / pageCount × 100)` clamped 1–100; paragraph bookmarks anchor to the first paragraph that starts on the page (`at = 0`). The last page is followed by the end matter as its own page; turning past it swaps the next chapter in at page 1.
G3. **Tap bands** (`mobile/lib/skins/glass/screens/novel/page_turn.dart` + test): `STANDARD` 25 / 50 / 25 % (back / menu / forward; novels always read left to right, so the bands never mirror); `BOTH_MARGINS` (both side bands go forward, the centre is menu); `ONE_HAND` (left 20 % back, the top 12 % across the full width menu, the rest forward). Test each preset at its band edges.
G4. **Slide** (default): the `PageView` uses `GlassPagePhysics extends PageScrollPhysics`, whose ballistic simulation projects `x + 0.499 × vx`, turns when the projection passes 50 % of the width, and settles on `springPage` with the release velocity; the turning page carries a `BoxShadow(blurRadius: 8, color: black at 0.45 × progress)` on its moving edge, read from `PageController.page`; `page.turn` (`selection`) and the `tick` sound on commit. Taps and keys turn with the same spring from rest.
G5. **Lift** (Glass's own): the `PageView` switches to `NeverScrollableScrollPhysics` and a horizontal-drag layer drives the turn. Forward: page n is drawn on top of page n + 1 with `Transform(transform: Matrix4.identity()..setEntry(3, 2, 1 / 1600)..rotateY(angle), alignment: Alignment.centerLeft)`, `angle` 0 → −100°; backward: page n − 1 rotates in from −100° over page n. Progress = drag distance / page width (release velocity included through projection); a 40 px `LinearGradient` band of `Color(0x2EFFFFFF)` (white at 0.18) crosses the turning page with progress; the page beneath un-dims from brightness 0.85 to 1 (`ColorFiltered` scale matrix); past −90° the page shows its back, the paper colour at 92 %. Release settles on `springPage`; on commit `PageController.jumpToPage(n ± 1)` and the overlay copy is removed; a cancel returns to page n. The turning copy is `IgnorePointer` + `ExcludeSemantics` + `SelectionContainer.disabled`, so selection stays on the live page. Pure transforms only: no `toImage`, no shader.
G6. **Fade:** a 160 ms cross-fade. **Reduce Motion:** Fade whatever the choice.
G7. **Hardware keys in paged mode:** `j`, Space, `→` forward; `k`, Shift+Space, `←` back; Home / End chapter start and end.
G8. **Pinch for text size** (`pinch_steps.dart` + test): a `Listener` counts pointers; while two are down the column's physics is `NeverScrollableScrollPhysics` and a `ScaleGestureRecognizer` in a `RawGestureDetector` drives `steps = trunc(ln(scale) / ln(1.15))` from the gesture's start, clamped 15–30, with `detent.tick` per step and `detent.limit` at an end; the text is never visually scaled: a `glassThin` capsule "Text size 21" shows top-centre during the pinch (it takes the chip slot of the budget), and the column reflows once on release around the paragraph anchor. Hardware `=`, `+`, `-`, `0` step and reset the same value.
G9. **Line guide** (option): two frosted bands (`BackdropFilter(ImageFilter.blur(sigmaX: 6, sigmaY: 6))` with a fill of the paper colour at 55 %) above and below the current line band (2 lines tall at the body line height); drag the band or tap above or below it to move it one band. It is a scrim, not a `SkinGlass` layer.
