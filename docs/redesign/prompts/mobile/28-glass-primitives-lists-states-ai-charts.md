## Scope: deliver every item below

Sizes are logical px (1 px = 1 pt) and minimums (`ConstrainedBox(minHeight:)` plus padding, never `SizedBox(height:)` around text); hit areas are `GlassFrame.hitMin(context)` (44 iOS, 48 Android) with at least 8 px between adjacent hit areas; every named move goes through `GlassMotion`; every haptic through `GlassHaptics.fire`; every live glass surface is a `SkinGlass` with its `layer` so the registry sees it; anything repeated per item is a content twin (`GlassTwin.content`: fill `Color(0x9E131317)`, 0.5 px rim `Color(0x38FFFFFF)`, the inner light, no backdrop read). Inside a T4/T5 host (read `GlassHost`), text follows the §2.1.2 mapping (`onGlass` at the role's size; text a spec dims by opacity uses `onGlass` at `wght` 460) and state glyphs sit on the backing disc; on black, slabs, `glassSolid1` and `glassSolid2` they stay bare. Every pressable is a `GlassPressable` (focus, hover, `ActivateIntent` on `Enter` and `Space`) inside `Semantics`. Transient errors are announced with `SemanticsService.sendAnnouncement(View.of(context), message, Directionality.of(context), assertiveness: Assertiveness.assertive)` (polite for status lines). Sections cited are `glass/DESIGN.md`.

### A. Lists (`primitives/list/grouped_list.dart`, `list_row.dart`, `plain_list.dart`, `chapter_row.dart`, `chapter_meta.dart`, §7.17)

1. **`GlassGroupedList`**: a `surface1` `#131317` container, `ClipRSuperellipse` radius 20, 16 px from the screen edges on phones (`GlassFrame.screenMargin` on wider frames); an optional section header above in `footnote` 13/600 uppercase +0.04 em `label2` with an 8 px gap; an optional footer below in `footnote` `label3`. Inside a T4/T5 host it draws no fill (rows sit on the glass body) and every text role maps to `onGlass`; on `glassSolid1`/`glassSolid2` it keeps `surface1` and the alpha labels. A sliver form (`GlassSliverGroupedList`) exists for `CustomScrollView`s.
2. **`GlassListRow`** anatomy: optional leading icon tile 30 × 30, radius 12 (`rIconTile`), an 18 px glyph in white on a semantic colour or on `surface3` `#222229`; title `body` `label1`; optional subtitle `footnote` `label2`; trailing value `body` `label2`; trailing control (a `GlassSwitch`, a 14 px `caret-right` in `g600` `#76767F`, a `GlassBadge`, or a button). Min height 52 (64 with a subtitle). Hairline separators 0.5 px `separator` `Color(0x8C545460)`, inset 16 px from the leading edge (60 px when the row has an icon tile). Below 360 px of row width (`LayoutBuilder`) or at text scale ≥ 1.6 the row stacks: title on its own line, meta below, trailing controls under the text, so nothing clips or scrolls sideways at the largest Dynamic Type size.
3. **`GlassPlainList`**: no container, rows separated by hairlines (notifications, bookmarks, sessions).
4. **`GlassChapterRow`**: min height 56 (68 with a secondary title). Leading: the chapter number in `mono` 15/20 `label2` with tabular figures (`FontFeature.tabularFigures()`), in a 44 px column; `·` for a null number, decimals shown ("12.5"). Title `body` ("Chapter 12" when the source gives none). Meta `caption1` `label3`: the date, "18/40" in `iris400` when in progress, "40 pages", "Read". Trailing: the download control (item G). Read rows drop their text to `label3`; the in-progress row gets a 2 px `iris500` bar under its number. `chapter_meta.dart` (tested): `chapterDateLabel(DateTime date, DateTime now)` → "Today", "Yesterday", "3 d ago" (2 to 6 days), "12 Sep" (older; "12 Sep 2025" in another year), and `chapterNumberLabel(double? n)`; reuse an existing formatter when `grep -rn "Yesterday" mobile/lib/features --include=*.dart` finds one outside `screens/` and `widgets/` that returns these strings.
5. **Press:** rows sink to a `surface3` fill and scale 0.99 on `springPress` (`GlassPressable(material: GlassMaterial.content)`).
6. **States (every row type):** default; hover `fill4`; pressed as above; focused (the `GlassFocusRing` inset 2 px, concentric with the row radius); disabled (text `label4`, not activatable, `Semantics(enabled: false)`); loading (a `GlassSkeleton` row of the same shape); selected (select mode: a leading 24 px `GlassSelectionCheck` springs in from the left on `springSnappy`, the row content slides right 36 px, selected rows get an `iris600` 14 % fill `Color(0x247563F2)`); error (a trailing `warning-circle` in `danger` with a `GlassTooltip`, or the error as the subtitle).
7. **Semantics:** each row is one `Semantics(button: true)` node merging its texts (`MergeSemantics`); select mode switches it (item D).

### B. Swipe row actions (`primitives/list/swipe_row.dart`, `swipe_math.dart`, `primitives/drag_owner.dart`, §7.34, §4.6, §8.0.5)

1. **Gesture:** a `HorizontalDragGestureRecognizer` owned by the row (a `RawGestureDetector`); the drag starts only after 10 px of horizontal movement (`thresholdDragSlopTouch`) with direction lock (a vertical-dominant move lets the list scroll), and never when the pointer went down within 24 px of the leading screen edge (the iOS back swipe owns that strip, §8.0.5). The row tracks the finger 1:1.
2. **Drag ownership** (`drag_owner.dart`, for `mobile/29`'s back swipe): `GlassDragOwner({required Widget child, required GlassDragOwnerKind kind, ValueListenable<bool>? atLeadingEdge})` registers its global rect in `glassDragOwnerRegistryProvider` on layout and removes it on dispose; `ownsDragAt(Offset global, {required bool movingRight})` returns true when the point lies inside an owner and more than 24 px from the leading screen edge, except an owner of kind `pager` or `rail` whose `atLeadingEdge` is true while the drag moves right. Wrap every swipe row (kind `row`), `mobile/26`'s `GlassRail` (kind `rail`, leading edge = scroll offset 0) and `mobile/27`'s `GlassTabPager` (kind `pager`, from `isAtFirstPanel`) in it. Test the registry with two rects.
3. **Actions:** `leading` and `trailing`, each `List<SwipeAction>` with `SwipeAction {String id, String label, IconData glyph, SwipeTone tone (iris | success | warning | danger | neutral), bool destructive, Future<void> Function() run, Future<void> Function()? undo}`; the first action of a side is its full-swipe action.
4. **Pills:** behind the row, separate pills 44 tall in 88 px slots, drawn as content twins; each inflates from scale 0.6 to 1.0 as it is revealed (`scale = 0.6 + 0.4 × clamp(reveal / 88, 0, 1)`), glyph 20 Regular and label `caption1` 600 in the action's colour (`iris400` `#A99BFF`, `success` `#3DDC84`, `warning` `#FFB547`, `danger` `#FF5C5C`, `label1` for neutral).
5. **Release:** the projected offset (`project(offset, velocity)`, §4.4) past half the tray width (`n × 88 / 2`) opens the tray on `springSnappy`; otherwise it closes on `springSettle`. One open tray per list (`GlassSwipeGroup` above the list closes the others); tapping the row, scrolling or opening another tray closes it; tapping a pill runs it.
6. **Full swipe:** a projected offset past 60 % of the row width (`thresholdRowCommitFraction`) stretches the leading pill to fill the gap and turns it solid in its colour (black label); `threshold.cross` fires when the projection crosses the line and `threshold.back` when it crosses back (the pill re-shrinks). Commit: the row slides out on `springDismiss` with the release velocity, the list closes the gap on `springSnappy` (`SizeTransition` driven by a spring), and the action runs; a destructive action shows the 10 s Undo toast (`showGlassToast(ref, GlassToastSpec(undo: …))`) instead of a confirm; a non-destructive one (mark read) lets the row spring back on `springSettle` once it ran.
7. **Alternatives (never gesture-only, §11, §14.8):** every row has a ⋯ plain icon button (hit `hitMin`, semantics label "More actions for {row name}") opening a `GlassMenu` with every swipe action; every action is a `CustomSemanticsAction(label: action.label)` in the row's `Semantics(customSemanticsActions:)`, so VoiceOver's actions rotor and TalkBack's actions menu reach them; with a hover pointer (iPad trackpad, Android mouse) the actions also appear as plain icon buttons at the trailing edge on row hover and on focus within; with the row focused from a hardware keyboard, `m` runs `markRead`, `d` runs `download`, `Delete` (and `Backspace`) runs `remove`, and `u` undoes the last one while its toast shows.
8. **Reduced motion:** tracking stays 1:1; the release cross-fades to the outcome over 150 ms; pills appear at scale 1.
9. `swipe_math.dart` (tested): pill scale by reveal, open or close by projection, the full-swipe line at 0.6 × width projected, the 24 px leading-edge rule.

### C. Drag to reorder (`primitives/list/reorder_list.dart`, `reorder_controller.dart`, `reorder_math.dart`, §7.35)

1. **Lift from the preview:** a 450 ms press on a row calls `showGlassContextMenu(…, kind: GlassPreviewKind.row, onPreviewDrag: …)` (`mobile/27`: the row at 1.02, `dimContext`, the menu, `longpress.open`); a forwarded drag of more than 10 px closes the menu, fades `dimContext` out over 180 ms and hands the row to `GlassReorderController.beginFromPreview(index, globalPosition)`: the lift is drawn in an `OverlayEntry` at the finger, scale 1.02 → 1.03 on `springPress`, the rim brightens to `Color(0x4DFFFFFF)`, shadow `0 12 28 Color(0x8C000000)`, `reorder.lift`. A trailing handle (plain icon, hit `hitMin`, `dots-six-vertical`, semantics label "Reorder {name}") starts the lift at once with no menu.
2. **Drag:** neighbours part around the gap on `springSnappy` (each item's offset through a `motor` controller); `reorder.pass` (a `select` tick, rate-limited by `GlassHaptics`) each time the item passes a slot; the drop settles on `springSnappy` with `reorder.drop` (`soft(0.5)`) and calls `onReorder(int from, int to)` (screens write the order: source pins, collections, collection members, manual library order, the download queue, profiles). `layout: GlassReorderLayout.grid` maps the pointer to a cell by the grid's measured column count.
3. **Edge auto-scroll:** inside the 64 px edge zone of the nearest `Scrollable`, `speed = 1200 px/s × depthIntoZone / 64`, applied per frame with `position.jumpTo`.
4. **Keyboard (hardware):** `Alt+↑` / `Alt+↓` move one slot; `Alt+Shift+←` / `Alt+Shift+→` move one slot horizontally in grids; `Alt+Shift+↑` / `Alt+Shift+↓` move to the top or bottom; each move is announced assertively: "Solo Leveling moved to position 3 of 12". Every row's ⋯ menu and its `customSemanticsActions` carry "Move up", "Move down", "Move to top", "Move to bottom" (WCAG 2.5.7).
5. **Reduced motion:** tracking stays 1:1; neighbours jump; the drop cross-fades over 150 ms.
6. `reorder_math.dart` (tested): the target slot from the pointer in a list and in a grid, the auto-scroll speed at 0, 32 and 64 px, keyboard moves clamped at both ends.

### D. Bulk selection and the floating bar (`primitives/select/select_mode.dart`, `selectable_group.dart`, `select_paint.dart`, `floating_bar.dart`, `bulk_toolbar.dart`, `bulk_copy.dart`, §7.35)

1. **Controller:** `GlassSelectModeController<K>` (a `ChangeNotifier` with `active`, `selected: Set<K>`, `lastToggled`, `enter([K? first])`, `toggle(K)`, `extendTo(K)`, `selectAll(List<K> visible)`, `clear()`, `exit()`); the Library passes its state through the shared `librarySelectionProvider` (wrap it, do not fork it). Entering: "Select" in the nav row or ⋯ menu, "Select" in an item's context menu (that item starts selected), `x` on a focused item from a hardware keyboard (`Shift+x` selects the range to it).
2. **Choosing:** each tap toggles. After the first selection, a drag across items paints a range (`select_paint.dart`: the paint mode is the new state of the first painted item; items under the pointer path take it; the list auto-scrolls in its 64 px edge zones at the item C3 speed). `Ctrl+A` / `⌘A` selects all; `Esc` clears the selection first, then exits. **Android back** (§8.0.5 rule 5): a `PopScope(canPop: false)` while select mode is active exits it and clears the selection.
3. **Assist chips** (`GlassChip.assist`) above the grid or list: "Select all ({n} visible)" (selects every item the current filters show, up to the loaded page of 200, then says so: "Selected 200 of 412 shown") and "None"; screens may add their own ("Next 10", "All unread", "Whole book").
4. **`GlassFloatingBar`** (the base for the bulk toolbar here and the "Unsaved changes" bar of `mobile/40`): a `glassRegular` (T3) capsule 52 tall, `SkinGlass(layer: GlassLayerKind.controls)`, one layer in the registry; while it shows it sets `glassBottomBarProvider` to `GlassBottomBar.bulk` (or `.unsaved`), which `mobile/27`'s overlay queue reads and `mobile/29`'s shell reads to hide the accessory. **Phone frame:** the accessory's slot, `bottom: safe-bottom + 21 + 64 + 8`, inset 21 px left and right. **Tablet and desktop frames:** `bottom: 24`, centred on the content column (its leading edge at `glassSidebarEdgeProvider`, 0 until `mobile/29` sets it), max width 720. It materialises in (250 ms) and dematerialises out (350 ms). While `MediaQuery.viewInsetsOf(context).bottom > 0` (the keyboard) it dematerialises and ignores input (`IgnorePointer` + `ExcludeSemantics`), and returns when the keyboard closes.
5. **`GlassBulkToolbar`**: "{n} selected" with `n` in `mono`; actions as `fill2` twin icon buttons (§2.4.2 rule 7) with visible labels on tablet and desktop frames and `GlassTooltip`s plus semantics labels on phones: Favourite (`star`), Unfavourite (`star-half`), Mark read (`check-circle`), Mark unread (`circle-dashed`), Add to collection (`folder-simple-plus`), Download (`cloud-arrow-down`), Remove (`trash-simple`, destructive, with Undo), and a plain "Done" that exits. Actions come from the screen as `BulkAction {String id, String label, IconData glyph, bool destructive, Future<BulkResult> Function(Set<K> ids, CancelToken cancel) run}` with `BulkResult {int ok, List<String> failed}`. 1. **`glass_reactions.dart`**: the six offered reactions in this order with their Phosphor Fill glyphs and stored kinds (`ReactionKind` from `features/circle/models/circle_models.dart`): Hype `fire` (`hype`), Love `heart` (`loved`), Wrecked `smiley-melting` (`wrecked`), Tears `drop` (`tears`), Twist `lightning` (`shook`), Masterpiece `hands-clapping` (`chefsKiss`); a stored `laughed` renders as "Laughed" with `smiley` Fill and is never offered. 7. **States:** sending (the flown glyph waits in the strip at 60 % opacity inside a 1.5 px `bloom` ring at 40 % until confirmed); failed (the glyph falls back to the button with the shake and the toast "Couldn't send your reaction"); offline (drawn at once and queued by the outbox); sharing off or `reactions` off (the strip still works, with the helper "Only you see this.
