# Mobile Cinematic The Numbers, the streak flame and The Annual (new feature 2)

Track: mobile · Order 60 · Depends on: `docs/redesign/prompts/mobile/20-cinematic-onboarding.md`, `docs/redesign/prompts/backend/03-stats-streaks-annual-listen-sessions.md` · Web twin: `docs/redesign/prompts/web/21-cinematic-numbers-streak-annual.md` · Proof folder: `docs/redesign/proof/mobile-21/`

## Goal

Build new feature 2 (reading stats and streaks) for the Cinematic skin in the Flutter app (iOS and Android), exactly as `docs/redesign/cinematic/DESIGN.md` §9.2 specifies. **The Numbers** at `/library/statistics` (Index branch, pushed with Page) is the magazine's back-of-book numbers: stat blocks with typed Bodoni numerals, chapters per day with the time line, the year heatmap, the 24-hour clock, the genre radar, where and what you read, recent sessions, your library, footnotes, and the ranges `7 DAYS · 30 DAYS · 90 DAYS · YEAR` as a swipeable contents-tab pager; charts are `CustomPainter`s with no chart library and a semantics node on every mark. **The streak flame** (on `flutter_animate` 4.5.2) is verified everywhere it appears, and the once-per-profile **milestone title cards** (7, 30, 100 and 365 days) open as an overlay route. **The Annual** at `/library/statistics/annual/:year` is a takeover on the root navigator: eleven story pages in a pager on phones, a centred 9:16 column at full height on tablets and landscape phones, previous Annuals, the end-credits colophon with `NARRATED BY`. The **press run** renders six share-card templates as widgets captured through `RepaintBoundary.toImage` to PNG and shared through `share_plus` 12.0.2, drawing only on the server's non-mature `shareable` fields, with `Save image` going to Photos through the share sheet on iOS and to `Pictures/ManhwaManiacs` through MediaStore on Android 10 and later. You deliver every state, reduced motion, screen-reader semantics, hardware-keyboard keys and text scaling. When you finish, the ScreenIds `numbers` and `annual` leave the Cinematic `PENDING` set. The web session runs the web twin in parallel; do not touch `frontend/`.

### F. Accessibility, motion and platform rules (§14)

- Charts: a text summary above each chart and a semantics node on every mark (§9.2.3); colour is never the only signal (heatmap levels by size, the selected bar also carries the readout).
- Hit targets: 44 × 44 pt on iOS and 48 × 48 dp on Android with 8 px spacing (range tabs, the bars' hit columns, the Annual controls, the press-run slug line).
- The double focus ring on hardware-keyboard focus only, never clipped; one `level: 1` masthead per screen; `SetHeading` section heads are `level: 2`.
- Text scale 1.0, 1.3 and 2.0: numerals cap at 1.15, the stat captions and folios grow to 2.0, rows grow (heights are minimums), nothing clips; the share cards ignore text scale (they are images).
- Reduced motion (§4.8): numerals appear whole (no typing, no caret); rules present at rest; the flame static (no flicker, ring rotation or sparks) and Ignite shows the filled glyph and bloom at once; the Annual never auto-advances and its colophon is a still page; Dip and Rise become 150 ms fades; the milestone card fades in over 200 ms; the tab rule fades between tabs; skeletons static at 0.8; programmatic scrolls jump; the leader dial and indeterminate rule keep running.
- Content safety (§14.11): share cards draw only on `shareable`; the Annual's own pages keep the full, gated data; no card names another Circle member.
