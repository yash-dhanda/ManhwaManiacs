# mobile/18 · Cinematic Settings, the edition picker and Stop the press

Step 54 of the redesign series (track `mobile`, group 5). Depends on `mobile/17-cinematic-downloads-index-status.md`. Its twin `web/18-cinematic-settings-and-edition-restart.md` builds the same cluster on the web and may run at the same time in another session; you never touch `frontend/`.

## Goal

Build Cinematic Settings in Flutter at `/settings` and `/settings/:section` (shell branch 4): on phones and below 900 dp a credits-list table of contents with folios and current values, each section a pushed page, and a full-screen settings search; from 900 dp (iPad, Android tablets) two panes with the search at the top of the table of contents. Deliver every section and control of `cinematic/DESIGN.md` §8.30.2 that the app has: Profile & account, Appearance with the **edition picker**, Reading: manga (with the app-only rows: keep screen awake, lock controls, volume keys, refresh rate), Reading: novels, Listen (with shake to extend), Ambient, Downloads & storage, Content with the 18+ certificate, Feedback (haptics with `Feel it`, UI sounds), Notifications with the per-profile `notify_enabled` master and the admin checker block, Keyboard (tablets), **Server**, Admin, **Diagnostics** (§8.30.7: FPS and jank, display, device, image cache, the layout grid and motion-timings switches, the `Edition (debug)` row) and About (version and build, What's new, the update or SideStore card, Licenses); plus the pushed pages Password & security, Members and Backup & restore. The edition picker plays each skin's Tonight as a bundled 36-frame PNG loop captured by the screenshot harness; while `Flags.glassAvailable` is false the Glass card is the disabled `NEXT ISSUE` plate and `flutter_dynamic_icon_plus` stays unregistered. The skin switch plays **Stop the press** (500 ms outgoing: rack out, blades close, masthead cuts in on black, then `AppRestart`), and the arriving Cinematic skin shows the 10 s undo toast. Every state, hardware key, haptic, reduced-motion variant and tablet layout is delivered.

### B. Settings structure (§8.30.1)

**Section registry** (`mobile/lib/skins/cinematic/screens/settings/settings_registry.dart`): an ordered list of `{slug, title, visible(ctx), rows}` feeding the search. App order and titles: `profile` "Profile & account", `appearance` "Appearance", `reading-manga` "Reading: manga", `reading-novels` "Reading: novels" (novels enabled), `listen` "Listen" (novels enabled), `ambient` "Ambient", `storage` "Downloads & storage" (`GET /settings` capabilities `client_downloads` true), `content` "Content", `circle` "Circle & privacy" (**registered by `mobile/22`**; not rendered here), `feedback` "Feedback", `notifications` "Notifications", `keyboard` "Keyboard" (only when `MediaQuery.sizeOf(context).shortestSide ≥ 600`), `server` "Server", `admin` "Admin" (admins), `diagnostics` "Diagnostics", `about` "About". Pushed pages: `security`, `members` (admin), `backup` (admin). Existing mobile paths `/settings/storage`, `/settings/backup`, `/settings/diagnostics` keep working. **Folios** are assigned at render to the visible sections in this order with no gaps (01 Profile & account … About last); the slugs are the stable ids.

**One pane (width < 900 dp: every phone, and tablets in portrait below 900):** `/settings` is the table of contents as a credits list (row 56 dp: folio, title, dot leaders, the current value as a folio where one exists: `Appearance ........ CINEMATIC`, `Reading: manga ........ STRIP`, `Downloads & storage ........ 4.1 GB`, `Feedback ........ HAPTICS ON`, `Server ........ manhwamaniacs.xyz`, `About ........ 3.5.0 (57)`; then `caret-right`). Each section is a pushed page (**Page**: in x +24 → 0 and fade over 320 ms `easeSettle`, out x 0 → −24 over 224 ms `easeLift`; the iOS `SwipeablePage` edge swipe) with a back arrow in the running head and its section header (§7.27, `SetHeading` `level: 1`, trigger `mount`). The search is a running-head `bare` `magnifying-glass` action that pushes a full-screen search list: the `compact` field "Search settings" autofocused, results (≤ 12, `matchSettings`): label in `type.ui` and the section title in `type.caption`; a tap opens that section and runs the jump below. Android back closes the search first (§8.0.5 back order item 3); on iOS it is wrapped in `PopScope(canPop: false)` with a visible `Close` (`quiet`). Empty: "No setting matches "{q}"."

**Two panes (width ≥ 900 dp, §8.0.9):** on the 8-column grid, the table of contents in columns 1–3: the `compact` search field at its top, then each section as a row: folio (`type.folio` `ink.45`) + title (`type.ui` `ink.60`); the current section `ink.100`, folio `spot`, a 2 px `spot` bar on its left edge, `Semantics(selected: true)`. The right pane (columns 4–8, controls max 720 dp wide) shows one section at a time: a section header (§7.27, H3 with the letter reveal, trigger `mount`), settings rows (§7.16, min 56: label `type.ui`, description `type.caption` `ink.45`, trailing control), then a section rule. `/settings` renders the first visible section in the right pane. Pushed pages render in the right pane under a `quiet` `← {Parent section}` link, and the parent stays lit. Search results drop down under the field in a list (`↓`/`↑` move, `Enter` opens).

**The jump** (both layouts): the pane switches to the section, scrolls to the row in 400 ms `durGlide` `easeSettle` (`Scrollable.ensureVisible`; `jumpTo` under reduced motion) and flashes a `spot.wash` band behind it (**Highlight sweep**: 200 ms `durClip` `easeSet`, then held 1200 ms `durHoldFlash`, then removed), and focus moves to the row's control.

**Footer** (end of the table of contents on phones, under the right pane on tablets): "Settings save as you change them." (the sentence "Changing the edition restarts the app." is added only when `Flags.glassAvailable` is true).

**No-profile guard:** sections that store per-profile values (appearance, both readings, listen, ambient, feedback's sounds, notifications' master, content) show a `NOTE` banner strip "No reading profile is active, so there's nowhere to save this yet." + `Choose a profile`, with their controls disabled.

**Server-backed sections** (content, notifications, the account block, security, members, backup, the edition row): *loading* (header renders; rows greeked at their exact heights with flicker; controls absent until data lands); *error* (a `CORRECTION` line under the header with the API message and a `quiet` `Retry` for that section only); *offline* (rows show their last known values with controls disabled and the caption "Needs a connection."). The edition row is the exception: its switch goes through the outbox, so offline it stays enabled and shows "Saves when you're back online." until the outbox flushes (only reachable once `glassAvailable` is true).

**Capabilities** (`GET /settings` → `capabilities`): `client_downloads` false hides Downloads & storage; `ocr` false hides dialogue rows; `collections` and `bookmarks` false hide their rows.

**Admin deep links:** a non-admin reaching `/settings/members`, `/settings/backup` or the admin block of notifications sees the notice `ADMINISTRATORS ONLY` with the section's name and `Back to Settings`; the controls never render.

**Hardware keys (tablets):** `/` focuses the settings search; `j` / `k` next / previous section in the table of contents; `Enter` opens it; `Esc` returns focus to the table of contents (and closes the search results first).
