## Goal

Build the Cinematic series pages in the Flutter app (iOS and Android, phones and tablets), per `docs/redesign/cinematic/DESIGN.md` §8.17, §8.18 and §8.19: the manga **Feature** page (the 4:5 phone hero and the tablet spread of §8.0.9 with the series' issue colour, credits enriched through `GET /series/enrichment`, follow, favourite, notify, the rating card, the `mature_override` radio menu, Mark read / Mark read up to here / Mark unread, Move to another source through repoint, the contents tabs, At a glance, DETAILS and the Lightbox), the novel **Book** page (typographic front matter and the windowed contents, with the N2 contents sheet in search mode on phones), and **chapter selection and downloads** on both (the picker with quick ranges, per-chapter download marks, the running state and summary, the series download card), all writing to the existing sqflite chapter store through `DownloadQueueController`.
