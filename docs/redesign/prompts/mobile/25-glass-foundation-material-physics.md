### F. Physics (`mobile/lib/skins/glass/physics/glass_physics.dart`, §4.3 to §4.6, §15.8)

Pure Dart, no widgets except the two classes that must be:

- `double project(double position, double velocityPxPerS)` = `position + (v / 1000) × 0.998 / (1 − 0.998)` (= `position + 0.499 × v`); `double projectCapped(double position, double v, double cap)` capped at one viewport length (`physicsProjectionCap` 1.0 × the length passed); `double nearest(List<double> points, double x)`; `double railSnap(double offset, double v, double stride)` = `(project(offset, v) / stride).round() × stride`.
- `double rubberband(double x, double d, [double c = 0.55])` = `d × (1 − 1 / (x × c / d + 1))` (sign-preserving for negative `x`); the reader's chapter-end pull uses `c = 0.35` (`physicsRubberBandChapterC`).
- `SpringDescription springOf(SpringToken t)` = `SpringDescription.withDurationAndBounce(duration: Duration(milliseconds: t.ms), bounce: t.bounce)` (mass 1, `k = (2π / d)²`, `c = 4π(1 − bounce) / d`); `double depthIntensity(int depth)` = `0.30 + 0.08 × depth`; `double impactIntensity(double v)` = `clamp(0.3 + |v| / 4000, 0.3, 1.0)`.
- `GlassTier tierFor(double shorterSide)`: `< 36 → t1`, `36–56 → t2`, `57–96 → t3`, `≥ 97 → t4` (`glassSnap` [36, 57, 97]; T5 is declared, never reached by size, §2.4.3). `double dimFor(double lb, {bool highContrast = false})` = `clamp(0.22 + 0.42 × lb, 0.22, 0.64)`, or clamped to 0.40–0.72 with Increase Contrast. `int gradFor(double lb, {bool bold = false})` = `round(40 × lb) + (bold ? 20 : 0)`. `double rondFor(double tier)` = `20 × tier` (fractional during growth).
- `extension CatchableSpring on AnimationController`: `({double value, double velocity}) catchMotion()` stops the controller and returns its value and `velocity` (the §4.3 catch; the caller fires `motion.catch` when it was animating); `TickerFuture springTo(double target, SpringToken token, {double velocityPxPerS = 0, double travelPx = 1})` runs `animateWith(SpringSimulation(springOf(token), value, target, velocityPxPerS / travelPx))`, reading the current velocity first when retargeting (§4.3 **Retarget**).
- `class SnapPhysics extends ScrollPhysics` (parent `BouncingScrollPhysics`): `stride` and `settle` (`springSettle`); `createBallisticSimulation` returns `ScrollSpringSimulation(springOf(settle), position.pixels, target, velocity)` where `target = railSnap(pixels, velocity, stride)` capped to one viewport of travel and clamped to the extents; out of range it defers to the parent (rubber band). About 20 lines. Used by rails, the voice orbit and pagers.
- `class Magnet`: `radius` 64 (`physicsMagnetRadius`), `pull` 0.35 per frame (`physicsMagnetPull`); `Offset step(Offset pos, List<MagnetTarget> targets)` pulls 0.35 of the remaining distance toward a target within 64 px and reports `captured` and `released` transitions (callers fire `magnet.capture` and `magnet.drop`).
- `mobile/test/skins/glass/glass_physics_test.dart` asserts every §15.8 value: `project(0, 1000)` ≈ 499 (±0.01); `rubberband(100, 800, 0.55)` ≈ 51.5 (±0.1); `springOf(SpringToken(ms: 520, bounce: 0))` → stiffness 146.0 and damping 24.17 (±0.1); `springOf(SpringToken(ms: 150, bounce: 0.14))` → 1754.6 and 72.05; the generated `GlassTokens.springPage` and `springTrack` give those same constants (a generated duration scaled by Cinematic's 1.2 factor fails here: report it for the shared track); `railSnap(310, 900, 136) == 816`; `tierFor(44) == t2`, `tierFor(240) == t4`, `tierFor(401) == t4`; `dimFor(1.0) == 0.64`, `dimFor(0) == 0.22`, and a dark cover with one white patch (mean `l` 0.2, `lMax` 1.0) read through the cover `Lb` source (item H) gives `dimFor(1.0) == 0.64`; `depthIntensity(3) == 0.54`; plus `impactIntensity(1200) == 0.6`, a `Magnet` capture at 63 px and none at 65 px, and `SnapPhysics` landing a 900 px/s fling from 310 on 816 within a 136-stride list.

### G. `skin_glass.dart`: the only wrapper around `liquid_glass_widgets` (§2.4, §2.5, §2.6, §3.5, §4.11, §15.3, §15.7)

1. **API.** `SkinGlass({required Widget child, GlassTierId tier = GlassTierId.auto, GlassFinish finish = GlassFinish.regular, GlassTwin? twin, GlassShape shape, double lb = 1.0, GlassRole role = GlassRole.chrome, bool overContent = true, Animation<double>? tierValue, ValueListenable<GlassGlow?>? glow, bool materialize = true, bool moving = false, GlassLayerKind layer = GlassLayerKind.controls, String? debugLabel})`. `GlassTierId` is `t1`…`t5` or `auto` (a `LayoutBuilder` applies `tierFor` to the shorter side); `GlassFinish` `regular | clear | tinted`; `GlassTwin` `content | onGlass | dense | cover | tinted`; `GlassShape` `capsule`, `circle`, or `superellipse(radius)`; `GlassRole` `chrome` (`GlassQuality.premium`), `pageControl` and `transient` (`GlassQuality.standard`); `GlassLayerKind` `controls | overlays | interruptions | hud` (z layers 3 to 6 of §2.4.1). `SkinGlassGroup({required List<SkinGlassShape> shapes, …})` draws the sibling shapes of one bar group in one layer (§2.4.1: the dock with its orb, the nav row's leading button, title capsule and trailing group).
2. **Renderer choice.** `GlassRenderer.liquid` when `docs/redesign/proof/mobile-03/glass-gate.md` recorded the liquid engine **and** `ui.ImageFilter.isShaderFilterSupported` is true; otherwise `GlassRenderer.frosted` (a renderer-capability rule, never a device tier). Record the gate's decision as `const GlassRenderer kGateRenderer` with a comment citing the record. The calibration page may override it for testing.
3. **Liquid path.** One surface is `AdaptiveGlass(shape:, settings:, quality:, useOwnLayer: true)`; a group is one `AdaptiveLiquidGlassLayer(settings:, quality:)` whose children are `LiquidGlass.grouped(shape: …)`. `LiquidGlassSettings` per tier from §2.4.3's **Flutter mapping**: `thickness` (T1 12, T2 20, T3 24, T4 40, T5 56), `blur` (2, 8, 10, 22, 32), `saturation` (1.4, 1.8, 1.8, 1.8, 1.7), `glassColor` = `foldDim(fill, dim)` (item 6), `lightIntensity` = `S` (0.55, 0.42, 0.40, 0.30, 0.26), `refractiveIndex` 1.2 (the 1.7.2 default, the calibration starting point), `chromaticAberration` 0 for T1–T3, 0.35 for T4, 0.5 for T5, `lightAngle` = the live angle in radians (item 22). Shapes: `LiquidRoundedSuperellipse(borderRadius: r)` (squircle, §2.3), `LiquidRoundedSuperellipse(borderRadius: height / 2)` for capsules, `LiquidOval()` for circles.
4. **Frost path** (`BackdropFilter` + `BackdropGroup`, stack risk 4): `BackdropFilter.grouped(filter: ImageFilter.compose(outer: ColorFilter.matrix(<saturation matrix for 1.8>), inner: ImageFilter.blur(sigmaX: blur + 6, sigmaY: blur + 6)))` clipped by `ClipRSuperellipse` (the §2.4.2 `frosted` recipe: blur + 6, saturate 1.8, no displacement, no dispersion), over the folded fill, with the painted rim and inner light of item 5. It requires one `BackdropGroup` per screen: the Glass root installs one now and the router's page wrapper installs one per route in `mobile/29`. In debug, a frost surface without a `BackdropGroup` ancestor asserts.
5. **Rim, inner light, shadow.** A `CustomPainter` (`GlassRimPainter`) draws the 0.5 px specular rim (1 physical px on DPR ≥ 2: `1 / devicePixelRatio`) as a `LinearGradient` along the light angle with stops `S` at 0, `0.06` at 0.35, `0.02` at 0.65, `S × 0.55` at 1.0 (white alphas), the first stop mixed with the field's rim tint at 18 % in OKLab (item H); on the liquid path it adds only what the library does not draw (check the 1.7.2 render against the checkerboard and keep one rim, never two). Inner light (T2 and up): a 1 px inset line along the lit edge at `S × 0.35` white and a 1 px inset shade along the far edge at `rgba(0,0,0,0.35)`. The tier's drop shadow (T1 `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.35)` … T5 `0 40px 96px rgba(0,0,0,0.66)`) is drawn only when `overContent` is true.
6. **Legibility dim** (§2.1.7). `Color foldDim(Color fill, double dim)` folds the `dimLegibility` layer (black at `dim`, beneath the fill) and the fill (colour `c`, alpha `a`) into one colour exactly for source-over compositing: `a' = 1 − (1 − dim)(1 − a)`, `rgb' = c × a / a'`. `dim = dimFor(lb, highContrast)`; it animates over `curveDimShift` (400 ms `Cubic(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`) with a `TweenAnimationBuilder`, and changes at once under reduced motion. An unknown backdrop is `lb = 1.0` (dim 0.64).
7. **Axes.** `GlassTextAxes(rond: rondFor(tier), grad: gradFor(lb, bold: boldText))` wraps the child; `grad` animates with the dim; during growth `rond` interpolates with the tier.
8. **Solid path** (`glassA11yProvider.solid`): no library widget, no `BackdropFilter`: T1–T3 fill `glassSolid1` `#1C1C22`, T4–T5 `glassSolid2` `#26262E`, a 1 px `Color(0x1AFFFFFF)` rim, inner light at `S × 0.5`, no dim, no caustic, no sweep, no dispersion, shadows as the variant replaced; `tinted` becomes solid `iris700` `#5B4AD1` (pressed `#4A3CB0`) with the same 1 px rim.
9. **Increase Contrast** (§4.11): `hcBorder` 1 px `Color(0x8CFFFFFF)` on every surface and slab, the dim clamped to 0.40–0.72, 3 px focus rings; `GlassA11y` exposes the label remaps (`label2` → `label1`, `label3` → `label2`, accent text `iris300` instead of `iris400`) for primitives.
10. **Content twins** (§2.4.1 rule 1, §2.4.2 rule 7): no backdrop read, no displacement, no dim, not registered: `content` fill `Color(0x9E131317)`; `onGlass` fill `fill2` `Color(0x4D787880)` with rim `Color(0x38FFFFFF)`; `dense` `Color(0xD1131317)`; `cover` `Color(0xDB000000)` with rim `Color(0x38FFFFFF)`; `tinted` twin fill `Color(0xDB7563F2)` (`iris600` at 86 %), rim `Color(0x4DFFFFFF)`, specular colour `Color(0x99E4DFFF)` (`iris100` at 60 %), no caustic. Each keeps the 0.5 px rim of the tier it replaces and the inner light.
11. **No glass on glass.** `GlassHost` (an `InheritedWidget`) marks descendants of a live surface; a `SkinGlass` built inside one without an explicit `twin` renders the `onGlass` twin (the host's lit action passes `twin: GlassTwin.tinted`). A test asserts no `BackdropFilter` and no `AdaptiveGlass` inside another.
12. **Registry and budget** (`glass/registry.dart`, §2.4.1, §15.7): every live surface (liquid or frost, not a twin, not solid) registers on mount and unregisters on dispose as a layer with its shape count (a group is 1 layer with n shapes), its `layer` kind and a `debugLabel`; scroll-edge and `dimContext` blurs register as `scrim`, counted separately. `glassRegistryProvider` exposes `{layers, shapes, scrims}`; in debug a seventh layer or a ninth shape logs one `debugPrint` naming every registrant. `GlassBudgetScope({required bool exempt, required String label})` exempts development galleries only.
13. **Two stacked layers at most** (§2.4.2 rule 7, §2.5): after each register, unregister and metrics change (post-frame), the registry computes each live surface's global rect; a surface overlapped by two live surfaces of higher layers that also overlap each other renders solid (`solid1`) until the stack clears.
14. **Materialise and dematerialise** (§2.4.2 rule 1, §4.10): on mount the refraction ramps 0 → 1 over `curveMaterialize` (250 ms; liquid: `thickness` and `chromaticAberration` × t; frost: blur σ × t) while opacity goes 0 → 1 over the first 120 ms; `SkinGlassState.dematerialize()` runs the reverse over `curveDematerialize` (350 ms) and completes a `Future` so callers can remove the surface afterwards. Reduced motion: a 150 ms opacity fade in (120 ms out) with full refraction from frame 0. `moving: true` holds the current settings (the library's geometry rebuilds only at rest).
15. **Press glow** (§2.4.2 rule 3): `glow` carries `{Offset local, bool on}`; a `RadialGradient` of radius 140 px at that point, `Color(0x29FFFFFF)` (tinted `Color(0x38BCB0FF)`), in over `curveGlowIn` 150 ms linear, out over `curveGlowOut` 60 ms linear. Glass never dims on press. Primitives (`mobile/26`) drive it.
16. **Growth** (§2.4.2 rule 2): with `tierValue` (1.0 to 5.0), every numeric tier parameter (thickness, blur, saturation, fill alpha, specular, shadow offset, blur and alpha, chromatic aberration, `ROND`) interpolates between the neighbouring tiers each frame (`lerpTier`, pure and tested).
17. **Focus ring** (§2.6, §14.4): `GlassFocusRing` paints, as a `foregroundPainter` on the widget that wraps the clipped glass (never inside `ClipRSuperellipse`), a 2 px `#000000` ring around the shape, a 2 px `iris300` `#BCB0FF` ring outside it (3 px under Increase Contrast), and a 6 px glow `Color(0x47BCB0FF)`, following the shape's radius; it shows only while `FocusManager.instance.highlightMode == FocusHighlightMode.traditional` and the node has focus; it appears over a 120 ms fade.
18. **Specular sweep** (§2.4.4): `GlassSweep.request(state)`: a 40 px band `Color(0x2EFFFFFF)` crossing the shape along the light angle from the lit corner to the far corner in 520 ms `curveSweep`, clipped to the shape; a coordinator allows one at a time and drops requests within 300 ms of the last start; it plays when a surface materialises and when a lit action becomes enabled. Reduced motion and solid: none.
19. **Caustic** (§2.4.4): `GlassCaustic`, a `CustomPaint` behind the one lit (`tinted`) object with `BlendMode.screen`: an ellipse 1.2 × 0.7 of the object's size, offset `causticOffset` 8 px along the light direction (down-right at 135°), `iris500` `#8F7EFF` at `causticAlpha` 0.14 (0.22 while pressed, in over 150 ms, out over 60 ms), `MaskFilter.blur(BlurStyle.normal, 18)` (`causticBlur`, σ); only when `overContent`; none under solid; no press brightening under reduced motion.
20. **Follow ring** (§2.4.4): `GlassFollowRing.play(bandRect, origin)`: a ring centred on the origin, radius 0 → the distance to the band's far corner, stroke 2 → 24 px, `iris300` at 30 % → 0, blur σ 6, `BlendMode.screen`, clipped to the band, over 700 ms `curveFollowRing`. Reduced motion: none.
21. **Accessibility scope** (§15.3): `SkinGlassRoot` installs `GlassAccessibilityScope(reduceMotion: ref.watch(glassMotionPrefsProvider).reduced, reduceTransparency: false, child: …)` above the Glass tree, so iOS Increase Contrast never turns glass into the library's frosted panel; `LiquidGlassWidgets.wrap()` is not used and `adaptiveQuality` stays `false`.
22. **Light angle** (§2.4.2 rule 5): `glassLightAngleProvider` (auto-dispose, so the sensor runs only while a Glass surface listens): `accelerometerEventStream(samplingPeriod: const Duration(milliseconds: 33))` (`sensors_plus` 7.1.0, 30 Hz), low-passed with α = 0.15, roll and pitch relative to the pose captured at the first sample after the screen subscribes; `angle = 135° + 25° × clamp(roll / 30°, −1, 1)`. A hover pointer in a desktop-frame window (iPad trackpad, Android mouse) drives it instead: `135° + 25° × (x / width − 0.5) × 2`. Paused while the app is not resumed. Pinned at 135° under reduced motion and when the per-device "Light follows the device" preference is off (default on; its switch is `mobile/39`'s).

### H. Palettes and `Lb` (§2.1.7, §2.1.8)

1. `mobile/lib/skins/glass/glass/palette.dart`: `coverPalette(item)` returns the server `palette {a, l, lMax}` when present; otherwise decodes a 64 px `ResizeImage`, runs `QuantizerCelebi().quantize(pixels, 16)` from `material_color_utilities` in `compute()`, applies the discard-and-rank rule itself (discard OKLCH `L < 0.12`, `L > 0.94` or `C < 0.025` unless all would go, then keep the two most populous; rank by `population × (0.5 + C)`; top three), never `Score.score`, and computes `l` (mean relative luminance) and `lMax` (95th percentile) from the same decode; cached per image URL for the session. `fieldColours(palette, mood)`: OKLCH clamp L 0.55–0.78, C 0.06–0.16, hue kept; a colour with C < 0.03 before clamping becomes the mood colour; fewer than three colours reuse the first (the renderer draws that blob at 60 % size). `rimTint(palette)`: `a[0]` at OKLCH L 0.86 with C ≤ 0.08.
2. OKLCH: if a conversion exists outside `lib/skins/` (`grep -rni "oklch\|oklab" mobile/lib --include=*.dart`), import it; if the only copy is inside `lib/skins/cinematic/`, move it to `mobile/lib/core/color/oklch.dart` in its own no-behaviour-change commit and import it from both skins (Cinematic's tests stay green); otherwise write `core/color/oklch.dart` (sRGB ↔ OKLab ↔ OKLCH) with a round-trip test.
3. `mobile/lib/skins/glass/glass/lb.dart`: `Lb` sources: `unknown` → 1.0; `field(l, opacity)` → `l × opacity + 0.02`; `cover(lMax)`; `page(PageSample, bands)` → the maximum of the listed `pTop`/`pMid`/`pBottom` (the engine's `PageSample`); `paper(luminance)`; `bar(field, rect)` → `max(field term, lItems)` where `lItems` is the largest `lMax` among registered items whose global rects intersect the bar's rect, recomputed on scroll at most every 100 ms and held while the scroll velocity exceeds 3000 px/s. Items register with `GlassLbItem(lMax:, child:)` (posters and rows use it in `mobile/26`).

### I. The ambient field (§2.1.8, §15.7)

`mobile/lib/skins/glass/glass/ambient_field.dart`: `GlassAmbientScope` + `glassAmbientProvider` (a screen declares `palette | mood | aurora` and an opacity) and `GlassAmbientField`, **one `CustomPaint`** behind content (z 0.5), no `BackdropFilter`, no `ImageFiltered`:

- three `RadialGradient` shaders, blob diameter 70 % of the viewport width at centres (18 %, 8 %), (78 %, 14 %), (46 %, 36 %); each blob's stops: colour at 0, colour at `r − blur / 2`, colour at 45 % of its alpha at `r`, transparent at `r + blur`, with `blur` 120 px on phones and 180 px on desktop frames; blob alpha = the source opacity (Home 26 %, library-type lists 18 %, series 28 %, the brand aurora `#8FD8FF`, `#A99BFF`, `#FF9ED8` at 20 %, moods at their own, the §2.1.8 table);

- **drift:** every 14 s each anchor takes a new random target within ±6 %, moved on `springDrift` (900 ms, bounce 0); frozen under reduced motion; paused under `TickerMode` false and while the app is not resumed;
- mood fallback before data: Romantic `#FF7AA8` 20 %, Action `#FF6B4A` 20 %, Comedy `#FFC94D` 16 %, Horror `#C0506F` 26 %, Slice of Life `#9FD98A` 16 %, Fantasy `#9B8CFF` 22 %, Default `#4336A3` + `#2B2370` 30 %;

### K. `GlassHaptics` (§5, §5.1, §5.2)

`mobile/lib/skins/glass/haptics.dart`: `GlassHaptics.fire(HapticEvent event, {double? velocity, int? depth})` maps each event to its §5.2 pattern from the generated Glass haptic map and calls `skin_haptics.dart`; it honours the per-device Haptics switch and `mm/platform haptics.systemEnabled` on Android (vibrator-path patterns skipped when 0); it rate-limits ticks (`selection`) to one per 40 ms (`physicsHapticMinInterval`) and impacts to one per 120 ms (`physicsImpactMinInterval`), a burst keeping the strongest; velocity-scaled events (`throw.commit`, `motion.catch` capped at 0.5) use `impactIntensity(v)` and, on Android, `haptics.oneShot {ms: 12, amplitude: round(i × 255)}`; `nav.push` uses `depthIntensity(d)` (and `max` with the velocity term for a thrown push). In debug builds it appends every fired event to `GlassHaptics.debugLog` (a 64-entry ring) so widget tests can count events. Test `mobile/test/skins/glass/glass_haptics_test.dart`: the two rate limits, burst-keeps-strongest, the switch off, and the intensity formulas.
