# Mobile Cinematic Circle (new feature 3)

Track: mobile · Order 62 · Depends on: `docs/redesign/prompts/mobile/21-cinematic-numbers-streak-annual.md`, `docs/redesign/prompts/backend/08-circle-core-sharing-presence.md`, `docs/redesign/prompts/backend/09-circle-reactions-letters-shelves.md` · Web twin: `docs/redesign/prompts/web/22-cinematic-circle.md` · Proof folder: `docs/redesign/proof/mobile-22/`

## Goal

Build new feature 3, the **Circle** (social for two or three readers), for the Cinematic skin in the Flutter app (iOS and Android), exactly as `docs/redesign/cinematic/DESIGN.md` §9.3 specifies, on top of the server half from `backend/08` and `backend/09`. Inside shell branch 4 (Index): the Circle screen at `/circle` (the magazine's letters page: the readers strip with the reading-now ring and `NOW` badges that follow the server's `now`, the activity column of dispatches, letters, shared shelves, a swipeable tab pager), and the member page at `/circle/:profileId` pushed from it. Across the app: **reactions on chapters** (the five Cinematic stamps plus `HYPE` and `WRECKED` in the same stamp style, the spoiler guard that seals reactions on chapters the viewer has not finished and unseals them on the reader engine's `chapterCompleted`, in the manga credits, the novel end matter, the page actions sheet, the reader `CIRCLE` tab and the feature and book pages), **Recommend to "Pass it on"** and the **letters** it sends (with `Keep`), **shared shelves** inside Collections, **Settings → Circle & privacy** (every switch), and the Circle rails on Tonight (`Sent to you`, `From the Circle`, `Most read in the circle`) with every badge and count. Every 18+ and isolation state is the server's (gate on serve); the client never shows a count, a placeholder or a hint for what it hides. You deliver phone, tablet and landscape layouts, reduced motion, screen-reader semantics, hardware-keyboard keys and haptics. When you finish, the ScreenIds `circle` and `circleMember` leave the Cinematic `PENDING` set. The web session runs the web twin in parallel; do not touch `frontend/`.

### C. Reactions on chapters (§9.3.3)

C1. **`mobile/lib/skins/cinematic/parts/reaction_stamps.dart`**: a row of square stamps 8 px apart. Each stamp is min 44 × 44 (48 × 48 on Android) with a 1 px `rule.2` outline: the glyph (Phosphor 20 Regular `ink.100`: `heart`, `lightning`, `smiley`, `drop`, `sparkle`) or, for `hype` and `wrecked`, the words `HYPE` and `WRECKED` in `type.micro` (a text stamp grows horizontally to its label + 12 px), then its count as a folio (`type.folio` `ink.60`) under the glyph; under each stamp the 20 px avatars of the members who pressed it (overlapping by 6 px, at most 3, then a `+2` folio). The five offered stamps always show; `HYPE` and `WRECKED` only when their count is above zero (pressable like the others). The viewer's own stamp (`mine`) is filled `ink.100` with a `#000000` glyph and count.
   - **Pressing** (`pressReaction`): the stamp translates 1 px down (80 ms `CineCurves.set`, back 160 ms `CineCurves.settle`), fills `ink.100`, its count types the new value at 50 ms per grapheme; haptic `reaction.send` (the `stamp` AHAP), sound `impress`. Another stamp moves the reaction; the same stamp clears it (`unreact`). Offline: drawn at once and queued in the outbox.
   - **Semantics** (§7 table): the row is `Semantics(label: 'Reactions to chapter 142', container: true)`; each stamp `Semantics(inMutuallyExclusiveGroup: true, checked: mine == kind, button: true, label: 'Loved, 3 reactions, selected')`; hardware arrows move between stamps and `Space` presses.
