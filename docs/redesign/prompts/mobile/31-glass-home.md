Track: mobile · Order 81 · Depends on: `docs/redesign/prompts/mobile/30-glass-auth-profiles-onboarding.md` · Web twin: `docs/redesign/prompts/web/31-glass-home.md` (runs in parallel) · Proof folder: `docs/redesign/proof/mobile-31/`

## Goal

Build the Glass skin's Home at `/` (ScreenId `tonight`) in the Flutter app (iOS and Android, phones and tablets), exactly as `docs/redesign/glass/DESIGN.md` §8.8 and §9.1.1 specify: the greeting typed at 50 ms per grapheme with its streak subline; the hero spotlight (up to six cards, a floating 2:3 card over its own blurred enlargement, the one lit action casting its caustic onto the cover, the device tilt of ±6°, one card per flick with projection, droplet page dots, the tablet and desktop stage) whose paging re-lights the whole screen (Light follows the story); Continue reading; the AI rails with the machine light and their `why` lines; throwing an AI card sideways for "Not interested" with Undo; dragging a poster onto a friend's orb to recommend it; the Updates bell with its count and pendulum; the ambient field following the story; the meniscus pull to refresh with `GET /home?refresh=1`; the Home items of the dock's long-press menu; and every state (loading, new profile, AI unavailable, offline, error, caught up, rate limited, stale, empty). Device tilt comes from the one accelerometer source `gravityProvider` (`sensors_plus` 7.1.0 at 30 Hz), subscribed only while Home is visible. The data comes only through the shared `homeFeedProvider` (`mobile/lib/features/home/`, built by `mobile/08`), extended here without changing Cinematic's behaviour. When you finish, `tonight` leaves the Glass `PENDING` set. Glass stays behind the debug row.

### G. States (§8.8, §7.24, §9.1.5)

1. **Loading:** the greeting shows at once (the name is local); after 180 ms a spotlight skeleton card (2:3 at 62 % width, radius 26; the stage skeleton at 440 or 360 px on wider frames) and three rail skeletons (a header bar and 5 posters).
2. **New profile** (onboarding skipped, nothing followed and nothing read): the "Start here" spotlight, the rails "Popular on your pinned sources" and "For you", and below them the inline `ObjectLens` (`LensSituation.library`, `books`) "Nothing followed yet" with the primary "Browse sources" (→ `Routes.sources`).
3. **AI unavailable:** AI rails show `AiNotice` with the reason's short line (and the fallback items); everything else works.
4. **Offline** (`origin == HomeFeedOrigin.offline`): the greeting, the shell's "Offline" capsule, one spotlight card for the most recently read downloaded series, and only "Ready offline" and "Continue reading" (downloaded only).
5. **Error:** a rail whose section failed shows `GlassRail`'s error card ("Couldn't load this row" with "Retry"); only when every section failed (`HomeFeedState.unavailable`) the whole screen is the full `ObjectLens` (`LensSituation.loadError`) "Couldn't load your home" with "Try again".
6. **Caught up:** "Updated for you" is omitted and the spotlight opens with "You're caught up" (A6).
7. **Rate limited:** `surfaceError` shows the `warning` capsule "Sources are busy. Retrying in 12 s" in the nav row with the automatic retry after `Retry-After`.
8. **Stale:** the AI stamps (D4).
9. **Empty** (`HomeFeedState.empty`): as the new-profile state.

### H. Keys and semantics (hardware keyboards; single-key bindings obey `glassSingleKeyProvider`)

`Ctrl+Enter` / `⌘Enter` continues the most recent read (the shell); `←` / `→` page the spotlight when it has focus; `↑` / `↓` move between rails; `Enter` and `o` open the focused item; `.` and `Shift+F10` open its menu; `p` opens Previously on for the spotlight's candidate card or the focused Continue stack; `r` refreshes; `Delete` marks a focused AI card Not interested; `g u` goes to Updates (the shell). Register Home's keys in the shared key registry (group "Home") so the shortcuts sheet lists them. The greeting is the only `Semantics(header: true, headingLevel: 1)`; each rail header is a heading; route focus lands on the greeting without skipping its typing.

### I. Budget and performance (§15.7)

At the top of Home on a phone the live glass layers are the nav-row group, the dock group (dock + orb) and the spotlight controls group (2 shapes): 3 layers, at most 7 shapes; scrolled, the controls become twins and the accessory joins the dock group: 2 layers; a sheet and a toast or menu on top stay within 4 layers and 8 shapes. Tablet and desktop frames: the sidebar, the toolbar group and the spotlight controls group, plus at most a toast and a menu: at most 5 layers and 8 shapes. Letter reveals: at most two at once (`reveal_slots.dart`); posters decode lazily below the fold; the hero field and the ambient field are gradients with no filter; the accelerometer runs only per C4.
