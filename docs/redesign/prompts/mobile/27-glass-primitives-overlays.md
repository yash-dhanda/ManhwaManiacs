## Goal

Build the second half of the Glass component catalogue in the Flutter app: the physics sheet as a go_router `Page` on `smooth_sheets` 1.2.0 (`GlassSheetPage`, `GlassSheetRoute`, `GlassSheetBody`, `GlassSheetPhysics`, `GlassSnapGrid`: peek 96 px, medium 0.52 of the screen height, large at screen − safe-top − 10, inset 8 px at partial detents, the device-corner radius on iOS phones, the `dimSheet` barrier `0x47000000` driven by the sheet's position, the finger tracked 1:1, the projected release on `sheetSnap`, the 60 px rubber band, dismissal by projection or 1,500 px/s, a present and a button dismiss that a touch can catch, pops from outside that run to completion, Android predictive back, the keyboard rule, the scroll hand-off, the visionOS recession of the page behind, and the tablet and desktop panel and window forms); alerts that bloom from their source control with `confirmAlert()`; toasts on one overlay host with the draining rim; the overlay queue that decides what may show; in-page tabs and pagers; sliders, the fill slider, the reader scrub rail and the speed dial; switches, checkboxes, radio lists, the selection check and the Stepper; menus and context menus (the bloom from the trigger, slide to select, the lift preview over `dimContext`, secondary click and `Shift+F10`); banners and inline notices (status, new-chapters and app-update capsules); the image viewer; scroll edges, scrollbars and fast scroll; pull to refresh with the meniscus that snaps at 100 px on `CupertinoSliverRefreshControl`; and the Manga | Novels content-mode switch. ### A. The overlay queue (`primitives/overlay_queue.dart`, §7.12, §7.30, §15.7)

A pure queue (`OverlayQueueState`, tested in `overlay_queue_test.dart`) behind `overlayQueueProvider`:

- **Blockers:** open menus and context menus, and on phones open alerts. While any blocker is open, every item of the queue (toasts, the global new-chapters capsule and the app-update capsule) waits and shows when it closes (the §15.7 budget: a toast waits while a menu is open, on every platform). An item already showing when a menu opens leaves on `springDismiss` (a toast's timer pauses) and falls back in on `springSnappy` when the menu closes (§7.12 **Top-band priority**). The "Offline" and "Syncing" status capsules are not in this queue: they replace the title capsule inside the nav row (§7.14).
- **Top band on phones** (one slot under the nav row): alert > toast > global new-chapters capsule > app-update capsule; a lower item waits while a higher one shows, and the new-chapters capsule re-appears when a higher one leaves.
- **Tablet and desktop frames:** toasts and the new-chapters capsule share one queue (a toast waits while the capsule shows); the app-update capsule waits while the bulk-selection toolbar or the "Unsaved changes" bar shows (`glassBottomBarProvider`, set by `mobile/28`'s bulk toolbar and by the "Unsaved changes" bar that `mobile/40` builds on `mobile/28`'s `GlassFloatingBar`).
- It exposes `requestSlot(kind)`, `release(kind)` and `registerBlocker()` returning a disposer.

### B. Sheets (`routes/glass_sheet_route.dart`, `primitives/sheet_scaffold.dart`, `primitives/recede.dart`, `transitions/predictive_back_detector.dart`, §7.10, §15.3 **Sheets**)

Build exactly the design of §15.3 **Sheets**:

1. **`SpringCurve`** (add to `physics/glass_physics.dart` with tests): a `Curve` whose `transform(t)` samples `SpringSimulation(springOf(token), 0, 1, 0).x(t × settle)` for the token's settle time, clamped so `transform(1.0) == 1.0` (settle times: `sheet` 447 ms, `dismiss` 378 ms, `sheetSnap` 342 ms, `settle` 414 ms, `zoom` 558 ms). Test: the sampled `sheet` curve stays within 0.5 % of 1 from 447 ms, and `transform(0) == 0`.
2. **Route.** `GlassSheetPage<T>` (a go_router-ready `Page`) whose `createRoute` returns `GlassSheetRoute<T> extends ModalSheetRoute<T>` with `swipeDismissible: false`, `transitionDuration` 447 ms and `reverseTransitionDuration` 378 ms. The route's animation moves nothing on screen: `buildTransitions` returns the child wrapped only in `GlassPredictiveBackDetector` (the copy of Flutter 3.44.6's private `_PredictiveBackGestureDetector` in `transitions/predictive_back_detector.dart`, which forwards `PredictiveBackEvent`s to `handleStartBackGesture`, `handleUpdateBackGestureProgress`, `handleCommitBackGesture` and `handleCancelBackGesture`; `mobile/29`'s `GlassPageTransitionsBuilder` reuses it). The barrier is `barrierBuilder`'s `AnimatedModalBarrier` whose colour is a `SheetOffsetDrivenAnimation` from offset 0 to the opening detent driving `ColorTween(begin: Color(0x00000000), end: Color(0x47000000))` (`dimSheet`), so it follows the sheet's position, not time; its `onDismiss` runs the button dismiss (item 4). Every sheet route (`feature`, `recap`, `circleMember`, `profileNew` / `profileEdit`, and every `?sheet=` id) becomes a `GlassSheetPage` in `mobile/29`'s router; this step builds the page and registers gallery routes `/dev/glass/primitives/sheet/:id`.
3. **Present (catchable).** `GlassSheetBody` builds its `Sheet` with a `SheetController` and `initialOffset: SheetOffset(0)`; a post-frame callback on the route's first frame calls `controller.animateTo(<opening detent>, duration: 447 ms, curve: SpringCurve(springSheet))` (the `sheet` spring, bounce 0.08). A touch starts a `DragSheetActivity` that replaces the animation, so the sheet stops under the finger and tracks it 1:1 from that offset, `motion.catch` fires, and the release goes through `GlassSnapGrid`. It grows out of the trigger when the trigger sits at the bottom of the screen (a Bloom from its rect: the page takes `originRect`), otherwise from the bottom edge. The `sheet.open` sound plays when UI sounds are on.
4. **Button dismiss (catchable).** The close button and a barrier tap call `controller.animateTo(SheetOffset(0), duration: 378 ms, curve: SpringCurve(springDismiss))` and do not pop; the listener of item 6 pops once the offset settles at 0. A touch during it catches the sheet the same way. The `sheet.close` sound plays when UI sounds are on.
5. **Pops from outside the sheet run to completion.** Android back (the 3-button bar, or a predictive back that commits), `Esc` from a hardware keyboard (`DismissIntent` → `maybePop`) and a go_router location change that removes the page pop the route first; `GlassSheetRoute.didPop` then runs the same 378 ms `animateTo(SheetOffset(0))` while the route reverses; `ModalRoute` ignores input on a reversing route (§4.9).
6. **Detents and snapping.** `GlassSheetPhysics extends SheetPhysics with SheetPhysicsMixin`, whose `spring` returns `springOf(springSheetSnap)` (k 223.8, c 26.33). `GlassSnapGrid implements SheetSnapGrid`: `getSnapOffset(layout, offset, velocity)` computes `projectCapped(offset, velocity, viewportHeight)` and returns the nearest of the detents the sheet declares (`peek` 96 px, used only by the listen mini-player expansion; `medium` 0.52 × viewport height; `large` = viewport − safe-top − 10 px); when the projected top edge falls more than 50 % of the lowest detent's height below that detent, or the sheet is at its lowest detent moving down at ≥ 1,500 px/s (`thresholdSheetDismissVelocity`), it returns `SheetOffset(0)`. `getBoundaries` returns `(SheetOffset(0), large)`. A `SheetNotification` listener pops the route (`Navigator.pop`) when the offset settles at 0; it is armed only once the offset first reaches the opening detent or a drag starts, and it never pops a route that is already popping.
7. **Above the top detent:** the §4.5 rubber band `rubberband(x, viewportHeight, 0.55)` capped at 60 px, applied in `applyPhysicsToOffset`.
8. **Haptics:** a `SheetUpdateNotification` listener fires `sheet.pass` when the offset crosses a detent during a drag; `sheet.detent` on settle after a drag; `threshold.cross` when the live projection crosses the dismiss line and `threshold.back` when it crosses back; `sheet.dismiss` maps to none.
9. **Geometry and material (driven values).** Inset 8 px (`sheetInset`) on all sides at partial detents with radius `SkinGlass.deviceCornerRadius(context)` on all four corners (the device-corner radius on iOS phones, 36 elsewhere); toward `large` the inset lerps 8 → 0 over the last 20 % of travel, the bottom corners go to 0, and the material cross-fades `SkinGlass(tier: t4)` → `glassSolid1` `#1C1C22` (a tall sheet becomes opaque and attached); `material: GlassSheetMaterial.monolith` (the listen full player, `mobile/37`) is `SkinGlass(tier: t5)` at `medium`, cross-fading to `glassSolid2` `#26262E` at `large`. All driven by `SheetOffsetDrivenAnimation(controller:, initialValue:, startOffset:, endOffset:)`, never by time.
10. **Grabber and header** (`sheet_scaffold.dart`): a 36 × 5 `fill1` capsule 8 px from the top inside a `hitMin` × `hitMin` button labelled "Sheet size"; a tap cycles the declared detents; with hardware-keyboard focus `↑`/`↓` change the detent. The 56 px header: title `title2` leading (centred for pickers), a trailing close button (the `fill2` twin circle 32 with ×, hit `hitMin`, label "Close"), an optional leading action. On open, focus moves to the title (a `FocusNode` on its `Semantics(header: true)`); on close it returns to the trigger (assert it in a test). The sheet is `Semantics(scopesRoute: true, namesRoute: true, explicitChildNodes: true, label: title)`.
11. **The page behind** (`recede.dart`): `GlassRecedeScope` exposes `ValueListenable<double> sheetProgress` (0 at `medium` or lower, 1 at `large`, following the lowest sheet's offset every frame, never time); `GlassRecede` wraps a page (the gallery wraps its page now; `mobile/29`'s shell wraps its root) and maps `p` to `Transform.scale(1 − 0.06 p)`, `ClipRSuperellipse` radius `12 p`, `ImageFiltered(ImageFilter.blur(sigma 8 p))` and a black overlay at `0.4 p` (60 % brightness at `large`). Partial detents show only the `dimSheet` barrier. Reduced motion: dim only, no scale, radius or blur.
12. **Scroll hand-off:** use `smooth_sheets` 1.2.0's scrollable integration (confirm its API name in the package source): an inner scroll view at its top hands a downward drag to the sheet; at a partial detent an upward drag expands the sheet before the content scrolls. The chapter list with 1,000 rows (`mobile/33`) uses the same body; test the hand-off with a 1,000-row fixture list here.
13. **Keyboard (phones):** a `Focus` listener in `GlassSheetBody` calls `controller.animateTo(large, duration: 342 ms, curve: SpringCurve(springSheetSnap))` when a descendant field gains focus; the field scrolls to 30 % of the visible height (`Scrollable.ensureVisible(context, alignment: 0.3)`); the content gets bottom padding `MediaQuery.viewInsetsOf(context).bottom`, returning to 0 on `sheetSnap` when the keyboard closes.
14. **Android predictive back:** `GlassSheetRoute` overrides `handleUpdateBackGestureProgress` to write the progress into a `ValueNotifier` that scales the sheet 1 → 0.94 and lifts it 12 px, instead of moving the route's controller; a commit dismisses as in item 5, a cancel returns on `springSettle`.
15. **Stacking:** at most two sheets; the lower one at `large` scales to 0.9165 and moves up 2 % of the screen height; a lower partial sheet drops to 70 % brightness; only the lowest sheet recedes the page. A third sheet replaces the top one (`pushReplacement`).
16. **Tablet and desktop frames** (shorter side ≥ 600), by the page's `wideForm`: `panel` (a right-side panel 440 wide, inset 12, radius 26, T4, entering from the right on `springSheet`, full height minus the 12 px insets, the 56 px header pinned and the body scrolling); `window` (a centred window 560 wide, radius 32, `tier: GlassTierId.auto` (T4 by its shorter side), blooming from its trigger on `springMorph`, centred on the content column, top `max(12 % of the height, 48)`, max height `min(80 % of the height, 880)`, pinned 56 px header, scrolling body); `detailWindow` (960 wide, radius 32, top 24, height = window − 48; a content-layer `glassMaterialThick` slab `rgba(19,19,23,0.84)` with blur 36, not live glass; the covered page recedes to scale 0.97, blur 8 and 50 % brightness by presentation progress); `popover` (the recap `offer`: a 420 px T4 popover blooming on `springMorph` from and anchored to its trigger). Behind panels, windows and the popover: `dimSheet`. `Esc`, a barrier tap and Android back close them. A panel or window opened from inside the detail window stacks over it (the two-sheet limit).
17. **States:** loading (skeleton rows inside, the header live), error (an inline error block with Retry), empty (the sheet's own copy), disabled actions greyed.
18. **`Lb`** for partial sheets, panels, windows and popovers: `max(field term, lItems under the surface's rect)` through `lb.dart`.
19. **Reduced motion:** present and dismiss become a fade plus a 16 px translate over 150 ms with no recession; a release settles with a 150 ms fade at the projected detent; the finger still tracks 1:1.

### C. Dialogs and alerts (`primitives/alert.dart`, `confirm_alert.dart`, §7.11)

- **Route:** `GlassAlertRoute<T> extends PopupRoute<T>` (`barrierDismissible: false`, barrier `dimModal` `Color(0x7A000000)` fading in over 180 ms).
- **Visual:** `SkinGlass(tier: t4, layer: interruptions)`, width 300 on phones and 420 on tablet and desktop frames, radius 26, padding 20; title `title3` 20/700 left-aligned; body `callout` `onGlass`; secondary lines `onGlass` at `footnote` size (never alpha labels: an alert can open over a reader page); an optional warning or danger box (a `surface2` slab with a 3 px leading bar in the semantic colour).
- **Buttons:** stacked full-width capsules (L 50) when there are three, or when two labels do not both fit side by side at M 44 (measure each label with a `TextPainter` at the current text scale; never truncate a label); otherwise side by side at M 44. Order: the least destructive first on top of a stack, and trailing when side by side. The destructive confirm is the solid `danger` variant (fires `delete.confirm`); the other buttons are the §2.4.2 rule 7 twins of their §7.1 variants; initial focus goes to the least destructive action.
- **Motion:** blooms from the control that opened it when there is one (scale 0.9 from that control's centre, `springMorph`, materialising; the caller passes `sourceRect`), otherwise from the centre (0.94 → 1 on `springMorph`); dismissal shrinks to 0.96 on `springDismiss` while dematerialising over 350 ms. Reduced motion: a 150 ms fade in place.
- **Behaviour:** `Esc` from a hardware keyboard and Android back cancel (`PopScope`), except while pending; focus is trapped in the route and returns to the trigger; semantics `Semantics(scopesRoute: true, namesRoute: true, explicitChildNodes: true, label: title, hint: body)`. It registers as a blocker on phones (item A) and calls `suppressLit()`.
- **States:** default; pending (the confirm shows its loading state; `PopScope(canPop: false)`; the barrier and back do nothing); error (an inline `onGlass` line above the buttons led by a `danger` `warning-circle` on the backing disc, announced assertively; the confirm shakes).
- **`confirmAlert(BuildContext context, {required String title, String? body, required String confirmLabel, bool destructive = false, Rect? sourceRect}) → Future<bool>`**; make it the default `onRequestConfirm` of `HoldToConfirm(mode: HoldMode.standalone)` from `mobile/26`, so a click on a standalone hold button always opens this alert with an explicit confirm button.

### D. Toasts (`primitives/toast.dart`, `toast_host.dart`, §7.12)

- **One toast system:** `GlassToastHost`, an `OverlayPortal` host (the gallery places it now; `mobile/29`'s shell places it at the Glass root), and `showGlassToast(ref, GlassToastSpec)` through the overlay queue (item A). No package.
- **Visual:** a `glassThin` (T2) capsule, min height 44 (two-line variant 60 with radius 22; at `f ≥ 1.6` it wraps onto further lines with radius 22 and never truncates), padding 0 16, max width 420; a leading 20 px glyph in its semantic colour on the backing disc; text `callout` `onGlass`; an optional plain action ("Undo", "View"); a trailing close button (× 16 px glyph, hit `hitMin`, label "Dismiss"), plus `Semantics(onDismiss:)` and a "Dismiss" custom semantics action, so the VoiceOver escape gesture and TalkBack's dismiss close it.
- **Position:** phones: top-centre at safe-top + 60 (under the nav row, which stays usable); while a toast shows, `glassToastShowingProvider` is true so the shell's top edge plateau extends to safe-top + 104 (`mobile/29`). Tablet and desktop frames: bottom-left, 24 px from the sidebar's edge (`glassSidebarEdgeProvider`, 0 until `mobile/29` sets it) and 24 px from the window bottom, rising to 88 while `glassBottomBarProvider` is true (24 + 52 + 12). Inside the readers (`glassReaderActiveProvider`): top-centre at 60 px.
- **Motion:** falls in from 60 px above on `springSnappy` while materialising (Toast fall); leaves upward on `springDismiss`. It is catchable: a vertical or sideways drag tracks 1:1; a release whose projection passes 40 px up or sideways dismisses it; a drag down holds it (the timer pauses while touched). Stacking: at most two; a newer toast pushes the older one down 8 px and scales it to 0.94 behind it. Reduced motion: 150 ms fades.
- **Timing:** 4 s by default, 10 s with Undo; with Undo the capsule's rim drains clockwise from 12 o'clock as a progress ring (a `SweepGradient`-masked rim painter), so the remaining time is visible. A hover pointer over it, focus inside it and a touch pause the timer. While `glassAssistiveProvider` is true (a screen reader is on), toasts stay until dismissed.
- **Undo:** `GlassToastSpec(undo:)` registers the action; `undoLast()` runs the last destructive action's undo while its toast shows and for 60 s after it leaves (the `mod+Z` hardware binding is registered by `mobile/29`); `undo` haptic.
- **Keyboard and a11y (hardware keyboards):** `Alt+N` moves focus to the newest toast; `Esc` dismisses the focused toast and returns focus where it was. Every toast is announced with `SemanticsService.sendAnnouncement` (polite; errors assertive).

### E. In-page tabs and pagers (`primitives/tab_pager.dart`, §7.13)

### L. Pull to refresh (`primitives/pull_to_refresh.dart`, `pull_physics.dart`, §7.33)

- Built on `CupertinoSliverRefreshControl(refreshTriggerPullDistance: 100, refreshIndicatorExtent: 60, builder: …, onRefresh: …)` with `BouncingScrollPhysics` on both platforms (the widget asserts in debug that its scroll view bounces). On Flutter the contract's "raw px" is the control's `pulledExtent`.
- **The droplet** (the builder): a `glassThin` droplet hangs from the top edge on a meniscus neck: its radius grows 0 → 16 px over the first 60 px; the neck width is `12 × (1 − progress)` px (progress = `pulledExtent / 100`), thinning as the pull continues; the `droplet` glyph inside rotates 360° per 100 px. At 100 px (`thresholdPullTrigger`) the neck snaps: the droplet pops free (`refresh.arm`; the `droplet` sound when UI sounds are on), springs to the 60 px rest line on `springLens` (k 223.8, c 20.94; the Meniscus refresh move) and becomes a liquid ring spinner while `onRefresh()` runs (`refresh.fire`, no haptic). Released before the trigger, it retracts into the edge on `springSettle`.
- **Done:** when `onRefresh()` returns `RefreshResult.changed` the spinner fills into a check (`refresh.done`; the caller runs the entrance wave for the new rows); with `RefreshResult.unchanged` it fades; then the list returns to 0 on `springSettle`. A polite announcement says "Updated", or the error.
- **Alternatives** (§11): screens add a Refresh item to their ⋯ menu and `R` on hardware keyboards; the widget exposes `refresh()` through a `GlassPullToRefreshController`.
- Reduced motion: a static spinner at the rest line once triggered.
- `pull_physics.dart` (tested): the radius at 30, 60 and 90 px, the neck width, and the trigger at exactly 100 px.
