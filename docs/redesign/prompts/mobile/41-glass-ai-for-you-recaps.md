# Mobile Glass AI: For you, Ask, recaps and More like this (new feature 1)

Track: mobile · Order 101 · Depends on: `docs/redesign/prompts/mobile/40-glass-you-about-admin-status.md` · Web twin: `docs/redesign/prompts/web/41-glass-ai-for-you-recaps.md` (runs in parallel) · Proof folder: `docs/redesign/proof/mobile-41/`

## Goal

Deliver new feature 1 of the owner's decisions (AI home and recommendations, "because you read X", similar series and the "previously on" recap before continuing a series or chapter) on the Glass skin of the Flutter app, exactly as `glass/DESIGN.md` §9.1 specifies, with the external AI always drawn in the **machine light** and always honest about waiting and about being unavailable. Build **For you and Ask** at `/library/recommendations` (ScreenId `picks`, with `?genre=`): the Ask box, the example chips, "Only my sources" and "Use my taste", the quota meter, the thinking orbit with its honest phase lines, the **dealt** answer cards, the For you and Because you read sections, Not interested (throw, swipe, menu, key) and More like this one; the **"Previously on" recaps**: the `mm.recap` modes Off · Ask · Always, the offer sheet that blooms out of Continue, the reader pill "Previously · 20 s", every explicit entry, the **recap deck** (route `recap`, a `GlassSheetPage` over the series detail, `shape=deck`, `scope=series|chapter`, consuming the server's SSE `section`, `delta`, `phase` and `done` events, words fading in as they arrive, the spoiler-guard footer, the compact chapter recap, keep-alive after close with the "ready" toast and the `recap.ready` haptic, How it works); **More like this** on series detail, the book page and the caught-up end card with the `fallback=genres` rail; and the **§9.1.5 one voice for "unavailable"** on every AI surface. Every state, phone, tablet and desktop frames, hardware keyboards, VoiceOver and TalkBack, and reduced motion. When you finish, the ScreenIds `picks` and `recap` leave the Glass `PENDING` map. The AI is called only by the server; the app never talks to an AI provider. Do not touch `frontend/`.
