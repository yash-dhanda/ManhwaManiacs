### 1. Motion additions (`mobile/lib/skins/cinematic/motion.dart` and new widgets)

- **Folio flip** (§4.5): `CineFolioFlip({required int value, CineTextRole? role})` renders a tabular Plex Mono number (`typeFolioLg` by default, `FontFeature.tabularFigures()`) whose changed digits roll: each changed digit 80 ms (`durTick`) with `CineCurves.easeSet`, digits 40 ms apart, old digit translating −100 % and fading, new digit entering from +100 %, each digit in a `ClipRect`; one accessible value (`Semantics(value: '$value')`, the digits in `ExcludeSemantics`). Reduced motion: instant. Used by the stepper here and by the running head and mastheads in mobile/06.
- **Insert** (dialogs): in 320 ms (`durColumn`) `settle`, a top-anchored reveal clip from `inset(0 0 100% 0)` to `inset(0)` (a `ClipRect` with a `CustomClipper<Rect>` whose bottom edge moves from 0 to the full height), barrier 0 → 0.78 over 200 ms (`durClip`); out a 160 ms (`durBeat`) fade with `CineCurves.lift`. Reduced: 150 ms (`durReduced`) opacity cross-fade.
- **Rise** (sheets): in 360 ms (`durRise`) `settle`, translate y 24 px → 0 plus fade; barrier 0 → 0.78 over 240 ms (`durLine`); out 240 ms `lift`, translate y +24 px plus fade; drag release `CineSprings.sheet.description` (576 ms, bounce 0). Reduced: 150 ms fade; a drag release finishes with a 150 ms fade.
- **Arm** (§7.10): a 2 px `proof` rule fills left → right under the destructive button over 1000 ms (`durArm`) `CineCurves.linear`. Reduced: no fill; the rule appears full at 1000 ms (the delay itself stays).
- **Lightbox** (§7.30): open 480 ms (`durSpread`) `CineCurves.turn` (the `Hero` match cut), close by button 320 ms `turn`, drag release `CineSprings.release.description` (504 ms). Reduced: 150 ms fade in and out.
- **Menu clip reveal**: 200 ms (`durClip`) `settle` from the anchored edge; exit fade 120 ms (`durSnap`). Reduced: 150 ms fade.
- **Toast**: in fade + 8 px rise 240 ms (`durLine`) `settle`; out fade 160 ms (`durBeat`) `lift`; stack push 240 ms `easeSet`. Reduced: 150 ms fade, older toasts move at once.
- **Highlight sweep** (§4.5): `CineHighlightSweep` paints one `spot.wash` band left → right behind its child over 200 ms (`durClip`) `easeSet`, bands 60 ms apart; reduced: the band at its end state at once. Needed by the current-row state here and by later screens.

### 2. Sheets (§7.9) — `sheet_route.dart`, `cine_sheet.dart`, `sheet_physics.dart`

- `CineSheetRoute<T> extends PageRoute<T>` exactly as §7.9 "Flutter: `CineSheetRoute`": `opaque => false`, `barrierColor => context.cine.scrimModal`, `barrierDismissible => true`, `barrierLabel` "Close", `maintainState => true`; one `AnimationController` measured **in pixels of sheet offset**, driven by a `VerticalDragGestureRecognizer` on the grabber and header and by the body's scroll overscroll at its top (a `NotificationListener<ScrollNotification>` that hands over to the drag when the body is at `pixels <= 0` and the finger moves down); detents: the content height capped at 0.92 of the screen by default, or `[0.5, 0.92]` for `livePreview: true` sheets; at scale ≥ 2.0 sheets open at the full 0.92 detent (§3.3); open Rise (360 ms `settle`), close 240 ms `lift`; release `controller.animateWith(SpringSimulation(CineSprings.sheet.description, offset, nearestDetent, velocity))`; dismiss when the sheet is below 30 % of its height or on a downward fling faster than 800 px/s; above the top detent the offset is `d · (1 − 1 / (x · 0.35 / d + 1))` with x the overdrag and d the sheet height (`scalarRubber`); Android back closes it (it is a route: the default pop), iOS swipe-down closes it (the drag). Because it is a `PageRoute`, a `Hero` can fly into it (Quick look, item 9).
- `sheet_physics.dart` (pure, tested in `mobile/test/skins/cinematic/primitives/sheet_physics_test.dart`): `double rubberBand(double x, double d)`, `double nearestDetent(double offset, List<double> detentHeights)`, `bool shouldDismiss(double visibleFraction, double velocityPxPerS)` (true below 0.30 or above 800 px/s downward).
- `CineSheet` scaffold: surface `paper.2` in `CineStock.raised`, radius 0, full width on phones and max 720 px centred from 600 px width (§8.0.9), 1 px `rule.2` top edge; grabber 32 × 3 `ink.30` centred 8 px below the top edge; header min 56 px: kicker (`typeKicker`) over title (`typeSubhead`) on the left, a `quiet` "Done" on the right (never an `x` in a sheet); body scrolls with `CineScrollBehavior`; bottom padding includes `MediaQuery.viewPaddingOf(context).bottom` and the keyboard inset.
- `Future<T?> showCineSheet<T>(BuildContext context, {required String kicker, required String title, required WidgetBuilder builder, bool livePreview = false, CineSheetState state = CineSheetState.ready})`.
- States: opening (Rise; haptic none; `SoundEvent.sheetOpen` → cue `sheet` when sounds are on); dragging (follows the finger 1:1); release (settles on the nearest detent with `CineSprings.sheet`, then `HapticEvent.sheetDetent`); dismissed by drag (`HapticEvent.sheetDismiss`, silent by map); closing; loading (the header renders, the body shows a 24 px `CineLeaderDial` and the kicker `LOADING` after 400 ms); error (a `CineNotice` inside the body).
- Focus: the route's `FocusScope` autofocuses the first focusable control on open, focus returns to the trigger on close (verify Flutter restores it; if it does not, store the trigger's `FocusNode` in the route and request it in `didPop`), `Tab` stays inside the sheet.
- Reduced motion: 150 ms fades for open and close; the drag still follows the finger and a release finishes with a 150 ms fade.
- **Widget test** `mobile/test/skins/cinematic/primitives/sheet_route_test.dart` (required by the plan): the route is not opaque and its barrier colour is `Color(0xC7000000)`; opening takes 360 ms and the sheet starts 24 px below its rest position at opacity 0; a drag of 29 % keeps it open and 31 % dismisses; a fling at 799 px/s keeps it and at 801 px/s dismisses; dragging 100 px above the top detent moves it by `rubberBand(100, d)` within 0.5 px; a release between detents settles on the nearest one through a `SpringSimulation` built from `CineSprings.sheet.description`; `tester.binding.handlePopRoute()` (Android back) closes it; tapping the barrier closes it; focus is on the first control after open; with `disableAnimations: true` open and close are 150 ms fades.

### 3. Dialogs and destructive confirms (§7.10) — `dialog_route.dart`, `cine_dialog.dart`, `cine_confirm_dialog.dart`, `arm.dart`, `inline_confirm.dart`, `commit_with_undo.dart`

- `CineDialogRoute<T> extends RawDialogRoute<T>` with `barrierColor: scrimModal`, the Insert transition in `transitionBuilder`, `transitionDuration` 320 ms, `reverseTransitionDuration` 160 ms, `barrierDismissible` true except while a destructive request is running.
- `CineDialog`: surface `paper.3` in `CineStock.raised`, 1 px `ink.30` border, radius 0, 88 % of the screen width on phones and max 560 px, centred (§7.10); title `typeSubhead` (Bodoni Moda); body `typeBody` `ink.60`, `CineMeasure(ch: 48)`; actions stacked full width at every width (the app is the phone frame): the committing action on top, `Cancel` as `quiet` below. Initial focus on the least destructive action (`Cancel`). `Esc` on a hardware keyboard and the barrier cancel.
- States: default; **arming** (destructive only, the first 1000 ms, the rule filling); **armed**; **pending** (the committing button shows its loading state, Cancel disabled, barrier not dismissible); **error** (an error line above the actions in `proof`).
- **The arm** (`arm.dart`, a pure state machine `ArmState` with `armedAt`, tested): every dialog, sheet or inline confirm whose committing action is `destructive` opens with that button disabled for 1000 ms (`durArm`); the 2 px `proof` rule fills under it (Arm); while it fills the label is `ink.30` and `Semantics(enabled: false)`; when full the label turns `proof` (or, for the final filled confirm, the fill turns `proof` with `#000` text) and `SemanticsService.announce('Ready', TextDirection.ltr)` fires; a tap, `Enter` or `Space` during the arm does nothing, so the double tap that opened the dialog can never confirm it; `Cancel` is live from the first frame. Pressing the armed button fires `HapticEvent.deleteConfirm`. The list of confirms that use it is §7.10's (delete profile, delete shelf, remove from shelf, remove downloads, remove saved audio, cancel all downloads, revoke a session, sign out, unfollow in bulk, clear shared activity, reset reader settings, reset offline storage, delete everything saved).
- **Heavy confirmations** (`CineConfirmDialog` parameters `typedPhrase`, `acknowledge`, `typedUsername`): a typed phrase field ("Type RESTORE to confirm", case-insensitive), an acknowledgement checkbox ("I understand this signs me out on this device too"), or a typed username ("Type {username} to confirm"); the confirm enables only when the arm has elapsed **and** the condition is met.
- **Inline confirm** (`CineInlineConfirm`): a destructive button that turns into "Confirm {verb}" with the arm, and reverts after 4000 ms without a press.
- **Single unfollow / remove never opens a dialog**: `commitWithUndo({required Future<void> Function() run, required Future<void> Function() undo, required String message})` commits at once and shows the toast "Removed {title}." with `Undo`, held 8000 ms (`durHoldToastAction`); pressing Undo fires `HapticEvent.undo` (cue `tick`).
- **Widget test** `mobile/test/skins/cinematic/primitives/arm_test.dart` (required by the plan): a double tap on the trigger opens the dialog and does not confirm; a tap at 500 ms does nothing; `Enter` at 999 ms does nothing; at 1,000 ms the confirm is enabled and "Ready" was announced (`tester.takeAnnouncements()` or a `SystemChannels.accessibility` mock), and a tap confirms and fires `delete.confirm`; `Cancel` works at 100 ms; a heavy confirm stays disabled at 1,500 ms until the phrase is typed (case-insensitive) and enables at once when it is; with `disableAnimations: true` the rule is absent before 1,000 ms and full at 1,000 ms. Plus `arm_state_test.dart` for the pure machine.
