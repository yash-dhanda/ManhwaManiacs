# Mobile Cinematic onboarding: "the first issue"

Track: mobile · Order 58 · Depends on: `docs/redesign/prompts/mobile/19-cinematic-ai-picks-similar-recap.md`, `docs/redesign/prompts/backend/05-ai-similar-recap-taste-onboarding.md` · Web twin: `docs/redesign/prompts/web/20-cinematic-onboarding.md` · Proof folder: `docs/redesign/proof/mobile-20/`

## Goal

Build the Cinematic onboarding takeover in the Flutter app (iOS and Android) exactly as `docs/redesign/cinematic/DESIGN.md` §8.7 specifies. A brand-new profile, right after the Iris closes on it in the profile picker, opens `/welcome?step=n` through the iris out instead of Tonight and sets its taste in four steps (Formats, Genres, Art style, Seeds; the Edition step 1 is skipped while `Flags.glassAvailable` is `false`, so the steps are 2 to 5 and the folio reads `1 / 4` … `4 / 4`). Every `Next` saves the answers so far with `PUT /profiles/{id}/taste {step, …}`, a killed app resumes at the saved `onboarding_step`, and `Print my first issue` follows the picks, saves the taste and plays **Cut to home**: the picked posters (at most 12) fly from the wall into Tonight's first section `01 Your first picks` in an `OverlayEntry` flight of `RawImage` copies while the rest of the wall fades to black, and Tonight then types its first headline. On the phones the steps sit in a pager that swipes **backward only** (forward needs `Next`), Android back goes from step n to n − 1 and from the first shown step back to the picker, and iOS wraps the route in `PopScope(canPop: false)` (§8.0.5). You deliver every state (loading, catalog unreachable, AI unavailable, offline, follow failures, `/home` failure, reduced motion), the phone, tablet and landscape layouts, hardware-keyboard access, screen-reader semantics, and the one skin-neutral data layer both skins use (`mobile/lib/features/onboarding/`, which Glass's `mobile/30` reuses unchanged). When you finish, the ScreenId `onboarding` leaves the Cinematic `PENDING` set. The web session runs the web twin in parallel; do not touch `frontend/`.

### D. Save and resume (§8.7 "Resume")

D1. Every `Next` writes the draft first, then sends `tasteBody(draft, next)` with `PUT /profiles/{id}/taste` in the background and moves on without waiting. A failed save is retried on the next `Next` and at Print.
D2. `Skip` (any step) sends `tasteBody(draft, done)`, clears the draft on success, and leaves by Dip to `/` with `context.go(Routes.tonight, extra: {'transition': 'dip'})`, where Tonight shows its "New profile, nothing read" state (mobile/08). A failed Skip save goes to the pending key (D5), silently.
D3. **Where onboarding opens.** Change mobile/07's picker: after the Iris closes on a profile whose `onboardingStep` is not `done` and that has no pending entry with `step: done`, the iris out opens `Routes.onboarding(step: resumeStep(...))` instead of Tonight. A profile whose pending entry says done is treated as done: send the pending `PUT` in the background (clear it on success) and open Tonight.
D4. **Restoring answers.** With `kTasteReadable`, `getTaste()` fills the answers on entry and the draft fills anything the server lacks; without it, the draft alone restores them. Answers chosen on another device stay on the server and are never overwritten, because only touched fields are sent (A8).
D5. **Pending done.** When the final `step: done` save fails after three attempts 2 s apart, store it in the pending key and finish the flow anyway. After Skip this is silent; after Print, the toast "Your picks are followed. Your taste will save when the server answers." (no action, so the default 3600 ms `durHoldToast`).
D6. The 18+ gate: every list comes from `GET /onboarding/catalog`, already gated by the server for this profile (§8.7). Nothing on screen mentions hidden content.

### E. Finish: "Print my first issue" and Cut to home (§8.7 Finish, §4.5 Cut to home, §13 moment 18)

E1. On press (haptic `tap.primary` = the `impress` AHAP, sound `set` if on): the wall dims to 30 % brightness (a `ColorFiltered` brightness matrix driven by a `TweenAnimationBuilder<double>` over 240 ms `durLine` `CineCurves.lift`), the 2 px `spot` indeterminate rule (§7.18, a 25 %-wide segment on a 1200 ms `durLoopRule` linear loop) runs across the content width under the headline, and "Printing issue No. 1…" types in `type.masthead` `ink.100` (`TypedHeadline(level: null)`) over the dimmed wall, centred in columns 1–4 (phone) / 1–8 (tablet). At the same time: `runFollows()` (4 at a time), the `step: done` save with the full taste (`formats`, `genres`, `styles`, and `seeds` as `{anilist_id}` for every pick in pick order), and a prefetch of `GET /home` through mobile/08's `homeFeedProvider` (`ref.read(homeFeedProvider(<current content kind>).future)` or its equivalent signature).
E2. When the follows have settled and `/home` has answered, the flight runs (skipped entirely under reduced motion, E5):
   1. The unpicked posters and the typed line fade to black over 240 ms `CineCurves.lift`.
   2. Before the route swap, for each poster in `flightList()`: read its global rect from a `GlobalKey` on the wall poster (`RenderBox.localToGlobal(Offset.zero) & size`) and its decoded `ui.Image` from the poster's resolved `ImageProvider` (an `ImageStreamListener` on `provider.resolve(ImageConfiguration.empty)`, keeping `imageInfo.image.clone()`), then call `cineFlight.arm(items)`. In the same frame the flight layer paints the copies at their source rects and the wall hides those posters (`Visibility(visible: false, maintainSize: true, maintainState: true, maintainAnimation: true)`).
   3. `context.go(Routes.tonight, extra: {'transition': 'none'})`. If mobile/06's Tonight page builder has no zero-duration branch, add one (a `CustomTransitionPage` with `transitionDuration: Duration.zero`) in `router.dart`. Tonight mounts at rest with section `01 Your first picks`, whose slots for the flying posters are hidden.
   4. Tonight's `first_picks` rail (mobile/08's section registry) gives each poster slot a `GlobalKey` registered as `flightSlotKey('{source_id}:{series_key}')`, hides the slots whose keys are in `cineFlight.items` while the status is `armed` or `flying`, and after its first layout with data (a post-frame callback) calls `cineFlight.land(targets)` with the slots' global rects.
   5. The flight: one `AnimationController` of duration 480 + 60 × (n − 1) ms; copy i animates over `Interval(i × 60 / T, (i × 60 + 480) / T, curve: CineCurves.turn)` from its source rect to its target rect (`Rect.lerp`, painted with `RawImage(image: …, fit: BoxFit.cover)` in a `Positioned.fromRect`). A slot outside the rail's visible scroll area (its rect's right edge beyond the rail's right edge) flies to the rail's right edge (same top, the slot's size) and fades to 0 over its last 160 ms. As each copy's interval completes, its slot becomes visible and the copy is removed (its cloned `ui.Image` disposed).
   6. After the last poster lands, the status becomes `landed` and Tonight plays its Front page moment for case 4b (mobile/08): the masthead kicker sets, the cover story racks into focus and its headline types "Tonight: start Omniscient Reader.", and the deck "Chapter 1 is waiting. The rest of your picks are below." sets. Tonight holds that moment until `landed`, rendering the headline as an invisible placeholder with the same text and style until then.
   Picks beyond 12 appear in the rail with its normal Set. The move is logged as `CineMotion.play('cutToHome', planned: Duration(milliseconds: 480 + 60 * (n - 1)))` so the motion-timings overlay shows it.
E3. **Flight plumbing.** `mobile/lib/skins/cinematic/flight.dart`: a Riverpod `Notifier` `cineFlightProvider` with `status` (`idle | armed | flying | landed`), `items` (`{key, ui.Image image, Rect rect}`), `arm(items)`, `land(Map<String, Rect> targets)`, `done()`, `reset()`, and `flightSlotKey(String key)` returning a stable `GlobalKey` per key. `mobile/lib/skins/cinematic/flight_layer.dart`: an `IgnorePointer` layer mounted once above the Navigator through the Cinematic skin's app `builder` (in `cinematic_skin.dart`, beside the toast host mobile/06 mounts there), so it survives the `/welcome` → `/` swap. Fail-safe: if `land()` has not been called 3000 ms after `arm()`, the layer fades its copies out over 240 ms `CineCurves.lift`, disposes them, resets, and Tonight plays normally.
E4. **Failures.**
   - Some follows fail: those picks are dropped from the flight; after it, the toast `followedToast(ok, total)` ("Followed 4 of 5. One couldn't be added.", error hold 6000 ms `durHoldToastError`, the `proof` edge).
   - Every follow fails (and at least one pick was available): no flight; the toast "Couldn't follow any of them. Try again from Discover." and a Dip to Tonight's new-profile state.
   - Only information-only picks: no follows and no flight; the taste is saved and Tonight opens by Dip.
   - `GET /home` fails: no flight; Tonight's error state appears after a Dip (mobile/08).
E5. **Reduced motion** (§4.8 row Cut to home): no dimming animation and no flight; the typed line appears whole; when the follows and `/home` settle, a 200 ms (`durClip`) cross-fade into Tonight (`extra: {'transition': 'crossfade'}`; add that branch to Tonight's page builder if missing), which then shows at rest.
E6. Glass chosen in step 1 is out of scope (step 1 does not exist until the Glass flip).
