Track: mobile · Order 85 · Depends on: `docs/redesign/prompts/mobile/32-glass-library-hub-downloads.md` · Web twin: `docs/redesign/prompts/web/33-glass-series-and-book.md` · Proof folder: `docs/redesign/proof/mobile-33/`

## Goal

Build the Glass series page in the Flutter app (`mobile/`, iOS and Android): the manga series detail of glass §8.12 and the novel book page of §8.13, one screen for both identities (`/sources/:sourceId/series/:seriesKey` and `/library/:followedId`), presented as a **sheet route** on phones (`medium` → `large`, the page behind receding by sheet position) with the `heroine` 0.7.2 poster zoom ("the cover lands", carrying the throw velocity), as the full-content-width window on tablet frames, as the 960 px `materialThick` two-column detail window on desktop frames, and as a full page when there is nothing beneath it (a cold deep link, a notification tap, the skin-switch return route). It carries the tinted band in the series palette with the **hero tilt** from the shared `gravityProvider` (`sensors_plus` 7.1.0 at 30 Hz, read only while the page is visible), the header, the split Continue button, follow, favourite and notify, the enrichment (score, format, official sites), the ⋯ menu (reading status, collections, tags with AI suggestions, check for new chapters, move to another source, content rating, open in browser), the download card with the phone-only note and Save to Files, the "Previously on" row and the More like this rail as entry points, and the chapter list of up to 1,000 rows and more with its download controls, select mode, the download picker, Mark read and Mark unread; on the book page the plate, the Literata front matter, the windowed Contents with the go-to field and the Book open move. Every state and every hardware-keyboard key is part of this step. When you finish, the ScreenIds `feature` and `featureByFollow` leave the Glass `PENDING` map. Glass stays behind Settings → Diagnostics → Edition (debug); Cinematic must not change by a single pixel.
