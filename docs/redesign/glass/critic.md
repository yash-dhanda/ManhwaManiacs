# Glass DESIGN.md ("Meniscus"): completeness critique

Reviewed 2026-09-28. All 3,690 lines of `glass/DESIGN.md` (§1 to §15 and Appendix A) were read and checked against `inventory/web.md` (32 screens, 503 rows, K1–K51, A1–A118), `inventory/mobile.md` (34 screens, 496 items, K01–K40), `inventory/capabilities.md` (111 endpoints, error envelope, rate limits), `inventory/00-decisions.md` and `stack-decision.md`. Where Glass says a value is "shared with Cinematic", `cinematic/DESIGN.md` was grepped to confirm it. Contrast figures below were recomputed with the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula.

The contract is very complete. Every inventory screen has a home. Nearly every screen lists loading, empty, error and offline states. The four new features have routes, entry points, states and backend fields. The motion, haptic and sound vocabularies are fully numeric. What follows covers only what is missing, vague, wrong or in conflict. Each row gives a severity, the evidence and a concrete fix.

Severity scale (same as `cinematic/critic.md`):
- **high**: blocks a release, breaks a binding owner decision or the stack decision, leaks 18+ content, or ships a required signature animation that does not work as written.
- **medium**: an implementer has to invent a behaviour, a state, a number or a data source, or two sections (or the two skins) disagree.
- **low**: polish, copy, naming or an edge case.

**Totals: 6 high, 42 medium, 24 low (72 items).** Rows that point at another row ("see X3") are not counted twice.
