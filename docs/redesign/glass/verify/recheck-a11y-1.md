# Recheck round 1: Glass accessibility, privacy and safety lens (`judge-a11y.md`, section "Confirmed")

Method: for each of the 31 confirmed findings (A11Y-7, A11Y-10 and A11Y-27 were refuted and are not checked) I read the edited sections of `glass/DESIGN.md` in full, not only the lines `fixed-a11y.md` names, and compared them with the judge's final fix. I then grepped for the old values the fix should have removed, and for every other place the changed rule or value is repeated, to look for contradictions. Where a residual is a contrast question I recomputed the ratio with the WCAG 2.x formula, compositing in sRGB as the judge did (backdrop, scrim, `dimLegibility`, tier fill). Where a fix touches existing code I checked the checkout (`frontend/public/sw-policy.js`: `pagesCacheName()` is `mm-pages-{RUNTIME_VERSION}` and `offlineCacheName(scope)` is `mm-offline-{CONTENT_VERSION}-{token}`, as §8.0.8 step 4 now says).

`DESIGN.md` was still being edited by other lenses' fix rounds while I worked (4,603 lines at the start, 4,606 lines and last modified 23:58:33 UTC at the end; a `recheck-web-2.md` round is in flight). I re-ran every anchor grep on the live file at the end. Section numbers are authoritative; line numbers are from the 4,606-line state.

Result: **25 resolved, 6 unresolved** (A11Y-3, A11Y-4, A11Y-11, A11Y-14, A11Y-18, A11Y-20). In all six the fix itself is in place as the judge wrote it. What remains is text elsewhere that still states the old rule or value and now contradicts the fix. Two of those residuals are also real contrast failures (A11Y-4: `label3` 3.64:1 in the command palette; A11Y-18: the followed bell 2.18:1 on a white cover). The other four are one-line or one-word consistency edits.
