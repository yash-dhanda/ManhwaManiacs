# Recheck round 1: Glass product lens (`judge-product.md`, section "Confirmed")

Method: for each of the 27 confirmed findings I read the edited sections of `glass/DESIGN.md` in full, not only the lines `fixed-product.md` names, and checked them against the judge's final fix. I then grepped for the old values the fix should have removed, and for every other place the changed value or rule is repeated, to look for contradictions. `DESIGN.md` was still being edited by other lenses' fix rounds while I worked (it grew from 4,586 to 4,603 lines), so I re-ran every anchor grep on the live file at the end. I also read `fixed-a11y.md` and `fixed-stack.md`, because two of their fixes deliberately replace parts of product fixes. Section numbers are authoritative. Line numbers are from the 4,603-line state and may have moved.

Result: **27 resolved, 0 unresolved.** Three findings are resolved with a documented deviation from the judge's text. In each case a later judge's fix replaced part of the product fix, and the file is consistent with that replacement:

- **PRODUCT-4 item 1:** replaced by A11Y-24.
- **PRODUCT-16, the `invalid_username` copy:** aligned with MOBILE-27.
- **PRODUCT-27, the tag shape:** replaced by STACK-24.

Seven non-blocking observations follow the table.

## Observations (not blocking; neighbours of the product fixes)

- **O1 (PRODUCT-4 paragraph).** In the §9.3.7 backend line, `DELETE /circle/activity` is still labelled "(Glass addition)" (l. 3433). After STACK-24, §15.5 and the §15.6 **Activity** row say it is Cinematic's, reused. The fix is to change "(Glass addition)" to "(Cinematic's, reused)".
- **O2 (PRODUCT-12).** §9.2.4 says every cover and title on a share side comes from a `shareable` block. Wrapped cards 8 (Busiest day, "that day's series fanned") and 9 (Firsts and lasts, two covers) draw from `busiest_day.series` and `firsts_lasts`. §15.5 makes those non-mature only, "matching Cinematic's `shareable` rule", so nothing unsafe is drawn. Still, the sentence and the two cards name different sources. Either name `busiest_day` and `firsts_lasts` as the two allowed exceptions in §9.2.4, or move both into `annual.shareable`.
- **O3 (PRODUCT-21).** The §7.15 bottom accessory's **Continue** variant reads "Continue Solo Leveling · Ch 143" (l. 1809), with no wording for an unopened next chapter. §8.8's "everywhere on Home" covers it in intent, but §7.15 is where the accessory is specified. It should read "Start Solo Leveling · Ch 143" when `page_count == 0`.
- **O4 (PRODUCT-1).** The §8.12 contract lists the controls it governs. The Home poster long-press menu also has "Mark read" (§8.8 Gestures, l. 2515), and it is not in that list. The rule for a series poster (every not-yet-completed `known_chapters` key) is stated only for the Library bulk toolbar. Adding "the Home poster context menu" to the §8.12 list would close this.
- **O5 (PRODUCT-25 item 1).** The novel reader now uses "Saved copy" for two different things. The §8.15.3 **Offline indicator** means a downloaded copy (l. 2778). The §8.15.7 `warning` capsule "Saved copy · 2 h" means a stale server cache (l. 2817). The tone and the age tell them apart, but the words mean two things. The §8.11 catalogue uses "Offline · saved copy from 2 h ago" for its offline case, so one option is to make the downloaded-copy capsule "On this device".
- **O6 (PRODUCT-13).** §9.3.3 opens the share sheet from "a collection's ⋯ → **Share** (owner)" (l. 3410), with no Auto exception. §8.18 disables Share on an Auto collection's detail with "Smart shelves can't be shared". The fix is to add "(not for Auto collections, §8.18)" in §9.3.3.
- **O7 (cross-lens record).** A11Y-24 and PRODUCT-4 gave opposite fixes for the Letters "Sent" filter. A11Y-24 wanted "Added" / "Delivered" with a server `added` field; PRODUCT-4 wanted "Not opened yet" / "Opened" and never "added". `fixed-a11y.md` records that the product version was kept because it is stricter, and the file follows it. No `added` field appears anywhere. Nothing to do. This note is here so the a11y recheck does not report it as missing.
