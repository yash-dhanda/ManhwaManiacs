# Recheck round 1: Glass mobile lens (`judge-mobile.md`, section "Confirmed")

Method: for each of the 27 confirmed findings I read the edited sections of `glass/DESIGN.md` in full (not only the lines `fixed-mobile.md` names) and compared them with the judge's final fix. I grepped for the old values the fix should have removed, and for every other place that repeats a changed value or rule, looking for contradictions. I also checked the cross-references to `cinematic/DESIGN.md` (§8.14.11 auto-queue, §8.23 `saveDownload`, §9.2.5 `mm/media`, §8.4 username rule): all exist and say what Glass cites.

Other lenses' fix rounds were editing `DESIGN.md` while I worked (it grew from 4,564 to 4,603 lines), so I re-ran every anchor grep on the live file at the end. Section numbers are authoritative; line numbers come from the 4,603-line state and may move.

Result: **25 resolved, 2 unresolved** (MOBILE-10, MOBILE-15). In both, the fix itself is in place. What remains is a sentence elsewhere that contradicts it, and each needs a one-line edit.

Four resolved findings differ from the judge's wording on purpose. Each is an alignment with a value or decision that already binds the document, so none should be re-applied:

- **MOBILE-1 and MOBILE-2:** the judge's 621 ms became 615 ms, the `page` settle in §4.2 and §4.10.
- **MOBILE-12:** API 34+ with `CONFIRM`/`REJECT` at 30+. `GESTURE_THRESHOLD_*` is API 34.
- **MOBILE-7:** the Wrapped half is now the a11y judge's reflowing column, not "Read this card".
- **MOBILE-27:** the per-code copy is the product judge's table.

## Observations (not blocking, outside the mobile findings' final fixes)

- **O1 (MOBILE-5 neighbour).** In the phone readers, toasts sit at "safe-top + 60" (§7.12 Position, l. 1763), and the "Previously" pill and the seam chip are placed relative to the top edge or the nav row. On Android in `immersiveSticky`, Flutter's safe-top (`viewPadding.top`) is 0, which is MOBILE-5's own premise. So a reader toast would land at y = 60 and overlap the top groups at `inset.top + 8` to `inset.top + 52` (32–76 px with a 24 dp status bar). One clause in §7.12 fixes it: "inside the phone readers: `inset.top` + 60 (§8.14.11)". On iOS the two values are equal.
- **O2 (MOBILE-7 neighbour).** §7.12 Toasts still uses a category name: "from text scale AX1 it wraps to as many lines as needed". §3.3 rule 1 says category names are replaced by `f` because Flutter cannot read them. It should read "from `f ≥ 1.6`".
- **O3 (MOBILE-8 neighbour).** §7.27 still lists the dock among the 150 ms tooltip delays ("dock and sidebar collapsed"). §3.3 now says the dock shows no tooltip at `f ≥ 1.6`. Tooltips are hover or focus only, so this is not the long-press clash MOBILE-8 fixed. §7.27 could say "sidebar collapsed" only, or "the minimised dock (pointer and focus only)".
- **O4 (MOBILE-20 neighbour).** With `ocrEngineAvailable` false, the §9.1.3 "no source text" lens hides its "Extract text" button, but its body still reads "Recaps are written from chapter text. Extract text from downloaded chapters, or just continue." A false-engine variant ("Recaps are written from chapter text. Chapters extracted on another device count too.") would match the §8.23 hint.
- **O5 (MOBILE-24 neighbour).** The §11 row "Long-press text" (novel reader) gives "the chapter's ⋯" as the alternative. The portrait novel reader has no ⋯: its top-right group is bookmark, listen, voices, Aa. Only landscape has one. The alternative should name a real control, for example "`b`; the Contents sheet".
- **O6 (MOBILE-6).** In landscape, the novel bottom capsule is "previous, percentage, next", and the ⋯ holds Voices and Soundscape. The novel cruise button (scroll mode, §9.4.1) has no landscape position; only `a` reaches it. This gap is in the judge's own text; the ⋯ could hold "Cruise" as the manga ⋯ does.
